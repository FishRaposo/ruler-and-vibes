## Approach

I reconciled the five sources by giving priority to the official maritime-safety report (S3) and the ferry operator (S2) over early wire reports (S1, S5), since the former are based on investigation rather than dockside impressions.

## Key decisions

- Accepted 51 persons (S2, S3) over 42 (S1, S5) because both the operator and official report corroborate the higher figure.
- Accepted 07:10 departure (S3) over 06:40 (S1, S5) because the official report is based on physical evidence.
- Accepted electrical fault (S3, S4) over engine fire (S1, S5) because physical inspection ruled out fire damage.
- Stated injuries are "not established by any source" since no source mentions them.
