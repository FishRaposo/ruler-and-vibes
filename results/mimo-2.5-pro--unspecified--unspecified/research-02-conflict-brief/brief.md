# Port Maren Ferry Incident — Reconciled Brief

A ferry near Port Maren experienced an onboard incident on the morning of the incident [S1][S5]. The vessel had 51 persons aboard at the time of departure [S2][S3], revising an early wire report of 42 passengers [S1][S5]. Departure occurred at 07:10 [S3], correcting an initial dockside estimate of 06:40 [S1][S5].

The cause was an electrical fault in the auxiliary generator, not an engine fire as initially reported by dockside witnesses [S3]. Physical inspection found wiring and insulation damage consistent with electrical faults, not the diffuse high-heat scorching characteristic of a genuine engine fire [S3][S4]. Dense smoke from this type of fault is readily mistaken for open flame, which explains the early witness accounts [S4].

Smoke was observed near the engine compartment shortly after departure [S1][S3]. The ferry operator's crew responded swiftly [S2]. The number of injuries is not established by any source.
