# Claim — A Two-Player Card Game

## Overview

**Claim** is a two-player market-building game. Players take turns claiming face-up cards from a shared market and stashing cards face-down in their stockpile. The goal is to build the highest stockpile total by the end of the game. The tension comes from timing: do you grab a valuable card now, or stash a card from your hand and hope something better appears?

**Components:** Standard 52-card deck (no jokers). Aces = 1, number cards = face value, Jacks = 11, Queens = 12, Kings = 13.

**Players:** 2

**Duration:** 10–15 minutes

## Setup

1. Shuffle the deck.
2. Deal **6 cards** to each player. Players may look at their own hand.
3. Place the remaining deck face-down as the **draw pile**.
4. Flip **4 cards** face-up from the draw pile to form the **market**.

## Turn Structure

On your turn, perform **both** steps in order:

### 1. Claim (choose one)
- **Take** one face-up card from the market into your hand. Then refill the market from the draw pile (if available).
- **Stash** one card from your hand face-down into your stockpile. (You do not draw or refill the market.)

### 2. Hand limit
If your hand exceeds 6 cards after your Claim, you must stash cards into your stockpile until you have exactly 6.

## Round End

The game ends when **both** conditions are met:
- The draw pile is empty.
- A player has no cards in hand at the end of their turn.

If the market is empty and you cannot stash (hand is empty), skip your turn.

## Scoring

Turn your stockpile face-up and sum the card values. **Highest total wins.**

Ties broken by most cards in stockpile. If still tied, the player who went second wins (slight first-player advantage is offset).

## Example Round

**Setup:** Alice and Bob are playing. After dealing, the market shows 7♦, K♠ (13), 3♣, 9♥.

**Alice's hand:** A♠, 5♦, 10♣, 4♥, 8♠, 2♦

**Bob's hand:** 6♣, J♥ (11), 3♦, 8♦, Q♣ (12), 2♥

**Turn 1 — Alice:** Takes K♠ (13) from the market. Market refills to 7♦, 10♠, 3♣, 9♥. Alice now has 7 cards; must stash one. Stashes 2♦ (2 points) to her stockpile. Hand returns to 6.

**Turn 2 — Bob:** Takes 10♠ from the market. Market refills to 7♦, 4♣, 3♣, 9♥. Bob stashes 2♥ (2 points). Hand returns to 6.

**Turn 3 — Alice:** Takes 9♥ from market. Market refills to 7♦, 4♣, 3♣, 6♠. Stashes 4♥ (4 points). Alice's stockpile: 2 + 4 = 6.

**Turn 4 — Bob:** Takes 7♦ from market. Market refills to 5♣, 4♣, 3♣, 6♠. Stashes 3♦ (3 points). Bob's stockpile: 2 + 3 = 5.

**Turn 5 — Alice:** Takes 6♠ from market. Market refills to 5♣, 4♣, 3♣, 2♣. Stashes 2♦ already in stockpile. Alice now has 7 cards; stashes A♠ (1 point). Alice's stockpile: 6 + 1 = 7.

**Turn 6 — Bob:** Takes 5♣ from market. Market refills to 4♣, 3♣, 2♣, 9♦. Stashes 6♣ (6 points). Bob's stockpile: 5 + 6 = 11.

The game continues until the draw pile is empty and both players have played out their hands. Bob's aggressive early stashing of mid-value cards gave him a stockpile lead, but Alice's strategy of hoarding high cards (K♠, 10♣, 9♥) in hand gives her options to stash big points in later turns.
