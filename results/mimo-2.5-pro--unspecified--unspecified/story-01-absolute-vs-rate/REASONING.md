# REASONING.md — story-01-absolute-vs-rate

## Approach

I computed complaint rates (complaints ÷ orders) for both regions and compared them, explaining why absolute counts are misleading given the volume difference.

## Key decisions

- Led with the rate comparison (1.5% vs 0.8%) rather than absolute counts, because the question asks about the "worse complaint problem" — a per-order metric is the correct measure of problem severity.
- Acknowledged the absolute count difference to show I'm not ignoring it, but reframed it as a volume effect.

## Trade-offs and limitations

- Kept it under 90 words as required.
