# Narrative

South has the worse complaint problem. Its complaint rate is 1.5% (90 complaints out of 6,000 orders), nearly double North's rate of 0.8% (192 out of 24,000). Although North generates more total complaints in absolute numbers, that is a function of handling four times as many orders. On a per-order basis, a South customer is almost twice as likely to file a complaint. The operations lead should focus improvement efforts on South's fulfillment or service quality before the higher rate compounds as volume grows.
