# Escalation Memo — Policy Conflict: DR-12 vs. AU-3

**To:** General Counsel
**From:** Compliance Analyst
**Date:** June 5
**Re:** Irreconcilable conflict between DR-12 §4.2 and AU-3 §7.1 for customer account closure logs

## The Conflict

DR-12 §4.2 requires purging customer activity logs 30 calendar days after account closure. AU-3 §7.1 requires retaining all activity logs for 180 calendar days after account closure, effective immediately, no exceptions. For this customer, these directives cannot both be satisfied.

## Relevant Dates

- **May 20:** Customer closed their account.
- **May 20 + 30 = June 19:** DR-12 purge deadline.
- **May 20 + 180 = November 16:** AU-3 retention expiration.
- **June 2:** Customer filed a formal deletion request.
- **June 2 + 30 = July 2:** Privacy commitment deadline for resolving the deletion request.

## The Problem

If we follow DR-12, we purge on June 19, violating AU-3. If we follow AU-3, we retain until November 16, violating DR-12 and potentially the privacy commitment to resolve the deletion request by July 2. The customer's deletion request adds urgency: we owe them a response by July 2 under our published commitment, but cannot resolve it without determining whether AU-3 supersedes DR-12.

## Interim Recommendation

Preserve all logs for this customer until a decision is made. This avoids irreversible deletion while the conflict is adjudicated. Respond to the customer acknowledging receipt of their deletion request and advising that it is under review due to an active audit requirement — without disclosing internal policy details.

## Requested Decision

Please advise by **June 19** which policy takes precedence, or whether a reconciliation (e.g., anonymizing logs to satisfy AU-3 while honoring the spirit of DR-12) is appropriate. This deadline ensures a decision before the DR-12 purge date.
