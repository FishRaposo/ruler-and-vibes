# Channel Metrics and Budget Allocation

## Computed Comparison

**Search channel:**
- Total spend: 500 + 500 + 600 + 600 = **$2,200**
- Total leads: 25 + 24 + 30 + 29 = **108**
- Total customers: 5 + 6 + 6 + 7 = **24**
- Cost per lead: $2,200 ÷ 108 = **$20.37**
- Lead → customer conversion rate: 24 ÷ 108 = **22.2%**
- Customer acquisition cost: $2,200 ÷ 24 = **$91.67**

**Social channel:**
- Total spend: 500 + 500 + 600 + 600 = **$2,200**
- Total leads: 50 + 55 + 62 + 58 = **225**
- Total customers: 4 + 4 + 5 + 4 = **17**
- Cost per lead: $2,200 ÷ 225 = **$9.78**
- Lead → customer conversion rate: 17 ÷ 225 = **7.6%**
- Customer acquisition cost: $2,200 ÷ 17 = **$129.41**

**Revenue per channel** (at $400/customer):
- Search: 24 × $400 = $9,600 → ROAS = 4.36×
- Social: 17 × $400 = $6,800 → ROAS = 3.09×

## Budget Allocation: $6,000

**Allocate $4,200 (70%) to Search and $1,800 (30%) to Social.**

Projected customers at current CAC:
- Search: $4,200 ÷ $91.67 ≈ **46 customers**
- Social: $1,800 ÷ $129.41 ≈ **14 customers**
- Total: ~60 customers → $24,000 revenue

Rationale: Search has a 29% lower CAC ($91.67 vs $129.41) and a nearly 3× higher conversion rate (22.2% vs 7.6%). Every dollar shifted from Social to Search produces more customers. However, Social generates 2× the leads at lower cost per lead, making it valuable for top-of-funnel awareness. A 70/30 split captures Search's efficiency while maintaining Social's reach advantage.

## Risks and Assumptions

- **Constant CAC assumed:** At higher spend, Search CAC may rise due to keyword saturation and diminishing returns. Social may scale more linearly.
- **No carryover assumed:** The four-week sample is small. Week-to-week variance could skew the averages (e.g., Search week 3–4 may show a trend).
- **Channel independence:** The model assumes channels don't interact. In reality, Social may drive branded searches that inflate Search's conversion rate.
- **Single-period value:** $400 is a one-time figure. If lifetime value differs by channel (e.g., Search customers churn faster), the CAC comparison changes.
