# SaaS Pricing Proposal — B2B Scheduling Tool

## Three-Tier Pricing

### Starter — $15 / user / month
- Up to 25 users
- Single-location scheduling
- Email notifications
- Basic reporting (weekly summary)
- Community support

### Professional — $29 / user / month
- Up to 100 users
- Multi-location scheduling
- SMS + email notifications
- Advanced reporting (real-time dashboard, export to CSV)
- Priority email support
- API access (read-only)

### Enterprise — $45 / user / month
- Unlimited users
- Multi-location + shift-swapping rules engine
- SMS + email + push notifications
- Custom reports and SSO integration
- Dedicated account manager
- Full API access (read/write)

---

## Break-Even Calculation

**Costs:** variable $6/user/month + fixed $8,000/month

| Tier | Price | Margin per user | Users to break even |
|------|-------|-----------------|---------------------|
| Starter | $15 | $15 − $6 = $9 | $8,000 ÷ $9 = **889 users** |
| Professional | $29 | $29 − $6 = $23 | $8,000 ÷ $23 = **348 users** |
| Enterprise | $45 | $45 − $6 = $39 | $8,000 ÷ $39 = **206 users** |

A blended mix (e.g. 40% Starter, 40% Professional, 20% Enterprise) yields average margin of 0.4×9 + 0.4×23 + 0.2×39 = $20.60/user, breaking even at 8,000 ÷ 20.60 ≈ **389 users**.

## Positioning

Our Starter tier undercuts the low-end competitor ($19) by 21%, capturing price-sensitive buyers. Professional sits between the two competitors at $29, offering multi-location and API features that neither competitor bundles at their price point. Enterprise at $45 undercuts the high-end competitor ($49) while matching on advanced features like SSO and full API access. This structure lets us compete on value at every tier rather than racing to the bottom.

## Assumptions
- Support costs are included in fixed overhead.
- No per-seat minimums or annual commitment discounts are modeled.
- Feature fences assume current product capabilities.
