# REASONING.md — business-02-pricing

## Approach

I placed all three tiers between or below the two competitor price points ($19 and $49) to create a clear value proposition. Break-even was calculated using the given variable cost ($6) and fixed cost ($8,000).

## Key decisions

- Starter at $15 undercuts the low competitor significantly to attract volume.
- Professional at $29 occupies the mid-market gap.
- Enterprise at $45 undercuts the high competitor while bundling premium features.
- Included a blended break-even to show realistic portfolio economics.

## Trade-offs and limitations

- Did not model churn, annual contracts, or usage-based pricing.
- Support cost assumptions are bundled into fixed costs.
