# REASONING.md — writing-02-registers

## Approach

I rewrote the paragraph three times, adjusting tone and length while preserving all four facts (cause, old date, new date, discount).

## Key decisions

- Formal version: concise, professional, passive-voice-free where possible.
- Friendly version: conversational, first-person, addresses the reader directly.
- Summary: 25 words or fewer, all four facts compressed into one sentence.
- Counted summary words: "Payment-system integration issues push the launch from March 3 to April 14; pre-order customers receive a 15% discount." = 21 words.

## Trade-offs and limitations

- Summary loses some nuance (no mention of "unforeseen" or "compensation") but preserves all four required facts.
