## Overview

**Siege of Suits** is a two-player trick game for a standard 52-card
deck. Each round, both players secretly commit one card to a shared
"Line," then reveal together. Higher rank wins the cards; ties feed a
growing shared pot that the next winner sweeps. Points come from
captured card ranks, doubled for one special suit. First to 21
points, or highest total when the deck runs out, wins.

## Setup

1. Shuffle the deck. Ace is low (value 1), King high (value 13).
2. Deal 13 cards face-down to each player as their hand. Stack the
   remaining 26 cards face-down as the **Draw Pile**.
3. Flip the top Draw Pile card face-up and set it aside, out of play.
   Its suit is the **Spoils Suit** for the game: captured cards of
   this suit score double.
4. Decide who leads first Round (Player A) by any fair method.

## Turn structure

Play proceeds as a series of **clashes**:

1. The leader plays one card face-down to the Line.
2. The other player plays one card face-down to the Line.
3. Both cards flip together. Suit never matters for winning a clash —
   only rank does.
4. **Higher rank wins:** the winner takes both cards into their
   face-down **Score Pile**. The **loser of this clash leads the
   next one**. (Losing costs you cards now, but hands you the choice
   of when to commit first next time.)
5. **Equal rank ties:** nobody captures. Both cards move to a shared
   **Pot** in the center instead, and the same leader leads again
   right away (no hand refill in between). The Pot carries over
   through repeated ties. Whoever wins the next non-tied clash takes
   that clash's two cards **plus the entire Pot**.
   - Safety valve: if the Pot ever reaches 8 cards (4 ties in a row)
     with no winner yet, split it evenly between both players, dealt
     one at a time starting with the current leader, then resume
     normal clashes with turn order passing to the other player.
6. **Refill:** right after any clash that resolves with a winner
   (not a tie), each player draws back up to 13 cards from the Draw
   Pile if any remain, leader first. (A tie clash draws no refill
   until the chain finally resolves.)
7. Every card must be played when it's your turn to add to the Line —
   there is no passing, discarding, or suit-following restriction.
   The only decision each clash is **which of your cards to commit**,
   weighing your hand's remaining strength against the size of the
   Pot and whether losing now (to lead next) sets up a bigger
   future win.
8. If a tie chain empties both hands before it resolves (no cards
   left to keep clashing), split the unresolved Pot evenly as in the
   safety valve above (leader first if odd), then end the game.
9. Otherwise the game ends when the Draw Pile and both hands are
   completely empty after a resolved (non-tie) clash.

## Winning

- Each captured card scores its rank (Ace=1 ... King=13); a captured
  card of the Spoils Suit scores double.
- After any resolved clash, if a player's Score Pile total is 21 or
  higher **and** the opponent could not possibly catch up even by
  capturing every remaining card in play (hands, Draw Pile, and Pot),
  that player wins immediately.
- Otherwise play continues until the deck and hands are exhausted;
  whoever's Score Pile totals higher then wins.
- If totals are exactly equal, whoever captured the single
  highest-ranked card during the game wins the tiebreak. If that is
  also equal, whoever won more individual clashes wins. If still
  equal, it's a draw.

## Example round

Players **Mara** (leads first) and **Theo**. Spoils Suit: Diamonds.

**Clash 1:** Mara leads 7-of-Clubs; Theo plays 4-of-Diamonds. 7 beats
4 — Mara captures both (worth 7 + 8 doubled = 15 at game end). Theo
lost, so Theo leads next. Both redraw to 13 cards.

**Clash 2:** Theo leads King-of-Diamonds; Mara plays King-of-Hearts.
Rank 13 vs 13 — a tie. Both Kings go to the Pot (2 cards). No refill
yet. Theo, the same leader, leads again immediately.

**Clash 3:** Theo leads 2-of-Spades; Mara plays 9-of-Clubs. 9 beats 2
— Mara captures the 2-of-Spades, the 9-of-Clubs, **and** the 2-card
Pot (both Kings): four cards this clash. Theo lost again, so Theo
leads next; both redraw to 13.

After three clashes, Mara's Score Pile is worth 7 + 8 + 2 + 9 + 26 +
13 = 65 points (King-of-Diamonds doubled as the Spoils Suit); Theo has
captured nothing. Play continues clash by clash — checking for an
early win past 21 after each resolved clash — until the deck and
hands empty and final totals decide the game.
