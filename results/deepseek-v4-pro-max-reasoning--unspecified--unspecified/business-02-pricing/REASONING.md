## Approach
Analyzed the cost structure, competitor landscape, and built a three-tier SaaS pricing model with clear feature fences that map to willingness-to-pay segments.

## Key decisions
Variable cost is $6/user/month, fixed costs $8,000/month. Competitors are at $19 and $49. I positioned the middle tier at $29 to sit between competitors while offering more value, with a low-end tier at $15 for price-sensitive teams and a high-end tier at $49 matching the premium competitor but with richer features.
