## Tiers

| Tier | Price/user/mo | Feature fence |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | $15 | Core scheduling, 2 calendar integrations, email reminders |
| Professional | $29 | Starter + unlimited calendars, team scheduling, analytics dashboard, priority support |
| Enterprise | $49 | Professional + SSO, audit logs, custom branding, dedicated account manager, SLA |

## Break-even calculation

Contribution margin per user: Starter $9, Professional $23, Enterprise $43 (price minus $6 variable cost). Fixed costs are $8,000/mo.

Assume a customer mix of 60% Starter, 30% Professional, 10% Enterprise. Blended margin = 0.6×$9 + 0.3×$23 + 0.1×$43 = $5.40 + $6.90 + $4.30 = $16.60/user. Break-even users = $8,000 / $16.60 ≈ 482 users.

If all users were on Starter: $8,000/$9 ≈ 889 users. All Professional: $8,000/$23 ≈ 348 users.

## Positioning

We sit between the $19 competitor (basic scheduling only) and the $49 competitor (heavy enterprise features). Our Starter tier undercuts the $19 player on price while still offering core scheduling. Professional at $29 gives more value than $19 for teams that need collaboration features. Enterprise at $49 matches the premium competitor head-to-head with comparable features. The gap between $19 and $49 is wide, and we fill it with a clear upgrade path.

Assumption: 60/30/10 mix is an estimate; actual mix depends on go-to-market channel and initial customer profile.
