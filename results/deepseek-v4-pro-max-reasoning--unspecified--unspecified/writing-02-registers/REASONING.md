## Approach
Extracted the four required facts from the source paragraph and rewrote them in three registers, each with a different audience and tone.

## Key decisions
- Formal version: removed passive constructions ("it has been decided"), tightened phrasing, kept professional tone suitable for investors.
- Friendly version: used conversational language ("we hit a few snags"), direct address ("your patience"), and simpler sentence structure.
- Summary: counted words carefully — 23 words, under the 25-word limit. Preserved all four facts.

## Trade-offs and limitations
The formal version omits the original's bureaucratic hedging ("due to the fact that") in favor of clearer prose, but all factual elements are preserved. The friendly version adds a direct "you" address which is not in the original but is consistent with a customer newsletter voice.
