## Approach
The question "which has the worse complaint problem" requires distinguishing absolute count from rate. Computed both regions' complaint rates: North = 192/24000 = 0.8%, South = 90/6000 = 1.5%. South's rate is nearly double, making it the worse region by complaint density despite having fewer absolute complaints.

## Key decisions
A narrative format (not a table or bullets) means explaining the numbers in prose. The key insight is that absolute numbers mislead — South looks smaller in raw complaints but carries far higher per-order risk. I framed the answer as a recommendation to the operations lead with a clear rationale.

## Trade-offs and limitations
Word limit (90 words) forced concision. I focused on the rate comparison and the actionable implication rather than restating all raw numbers.
