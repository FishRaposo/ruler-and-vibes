## Risks and Assumptions

- **Constant returns assumed**: this analysis extrapolates the four-week averages linearly. In practice, Search may face diminishing returns as the most convertible audience saturates.
- **Channel scalability**: Search spend at $6,000/month is 2.7x the current $2,200/month level. The channel may not scale linearly — keyword auction prices may rise.
- **Customer value stability**: $400 per customer is assumed stable; if Search customers differ from Social customers in downstream behavior (retention, upsells), the ROI comparison shifts.
- **Zero Social allocation risk**: cutting Social entirely loses all learnings from that channel. A small test budget (e.g. $500) could preserve optionality if Search performance degrades.
