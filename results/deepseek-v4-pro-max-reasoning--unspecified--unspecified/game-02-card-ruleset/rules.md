## Overview
Zone Hold is a two-player territory game. Each player builds valued sequences in personal zones. The player with the higher total value across their best 5 zones wins.

## Setup
Shuffle and deal 7 cards to each player. Place the top 5 cards of the deck face-up in a row between players — this is the market. Place the remaining deck as a draw pile. Each player has 5 empty zone slots in front of them.

## Turn structure
On your turn, do exactly ONE of:
1. **Draw**: take the top card of the deck into your hand.
2. **Play**: place a card from your hand into one of your empty zones, OR onto an existing zone if the new card's rank is higher than the top card and matches suit.
3. **Market**: take one face-up market card into your hand, then replace it from the deck.
Hand limit is 10 cards. If you exceed, discard down to 10 immediately.

## Winning
When the last zone is filled by either player, finish the round so both players have equal turns. Score: each zone is worth the sum of card ranks (Ace=1, 2-10 face, J=11, Q=12, K=13). If a zone has 3+ cards of the same suit, add +5 bonus. Total your 5 best zones. Highest total wins. Ties broken by most zones with bonus, then most total zones, then highest single zone.

## Example round
Anna and Ben play. Market: 7h, Qs, 3c, 9d, Kh. Anna draws. Ben plays 8s into zone 1. Anna plays 2d into zone 1. Ben markets: takes Qs. Anna plays Ah then 4h into zone 2 (same suit, building). Ben plays Ks into zone 2. Anna plays 10h onto zone 2 over 4h (higher rank, matching suit). After 5 rounds of filling, Anna scores zone 2 at Ah+4h+10h+5 bonus = 20. Ben's zone 1: 8s = 8. Anna wins 62-48.
