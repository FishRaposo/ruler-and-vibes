## Account

At approximately 06:40 on the morning of the incident, a ferry near Port Maren experienced an onboard emergency shortly after departure [S1][S3]. The official maritime-safety report later confirmed the vessel departed at 07:10 [S3], not 06:40 as initially reported by dockside witnesses [S1]. The ferry operator subsequently stated 51 persons were aboard [S2], a figure the official investigation corroborated [S3], revising the initial wire report's figure of 42 [S1][S5].

The cause was an electrical fault in the auxiliary generator [S3], not an engine fire as early dock observers believed [S1]. An independent marine engineer reviewing the report confirmed that auxiliary generator faults routinely produce dense smoke with minimal visible flame, consistent with witness accounts of smoke and apparent fire [S4]. Physical inspection found no fire damage consistent with a genuine engine fire [S3].

The number of injuries is not established by any source. Neither the operator's statement [S2] nor the official report [S3] nor any other source provides injury figures.
