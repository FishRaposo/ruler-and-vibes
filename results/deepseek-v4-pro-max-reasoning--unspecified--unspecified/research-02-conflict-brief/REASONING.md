## Approach
Read all five sources, identified three disputed points (number aboard, departure time, cause) and four required facts (add injury count as a fourth). Resolved disputes by preferring later, more authoritative sources (official report over wire dispatch; operator statement over early news). Used S4 only to corroborate the electrical-fault finding, not as an independent primary source.

## Key decisions
The official maritime-safety report [S3] carries the most weight because it is based on physical inspection and post-incident interviews, unlike [S1] which was filed the morning of the incident from dockside witnesses. The ferry operator [S2] specified 51 aboard but did not state a cause — I used [S2] for headcount and [S3] for cause and departure time. S5 is purely derivative of S1 and adds no new facts.

## Trade-offs and limitations
The 300-450 word constraint required compression of the narrative and the "Disputed points" table rationale. Injuries are genuinely absent from all five sources, so "not established by any source" is the honest answer.
