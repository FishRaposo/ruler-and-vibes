SUBJECT: Unresolvable policy conflict — DR-12 vs. AU-3 for closed account

Two company policies directly conflict for a customer who closed their account on May 20. I am escalating for a decision.

**The conflict**

Data Retention Policy DR-12 §4.2 requires purging all customer activity logs 30 days after account closure. Audit Readiness Policy AU-3 §7.1 (effective immediately) requires retaining all activity logs for 180 days after account closure with no exceptions. Both cannot be satisfied.

**Relevant dates**

- Account closed: May 20.
- 30 days after closure (DR-12 purge deadline): June 19.
- Deletion request received: June 2.
- 30-day privacy-commitment deadline for deletion resolution: July 2.
- 180 days after closure (AU-3 retention deadline): November 16.

**Current status**

The logs still exist. Under AU-3 the logs must be kept until November 16. Under DR-12 they must be purged by June 19. The privacy commitment requires action on the deletion request by July 2.

**Interim recommendation**

Retain the logs past June 19. A premature purge violates AU-3 and cannot be undone. A delayed purge violates DR-12 but can be corrected later if General Counsel resolves the conflict in DR-12's favor. This preserves both options. I recommend not responding to the deletion request until the policy conflict is resolved, to avoid committing to an action that one policy or the other will retroactively prohibit.

**Decision needed**

By June 12 — one week before the DR-12 purge deadline — I need a written determination of which policy takes precedence for this account, or instructions on an interim handling procedure (e.g., a limited-scope exception to one policy pending a broader policy review).
