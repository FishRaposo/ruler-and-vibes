## Task

Design a two-player game using ONLY a standard pair of six-sided dice (2
dice, and nothing else — no board, no counters, no pen and paper,
nothing printed or added). It must involve meaningful decisions — not
pure luck — and a match should take 10–20 minutes.

## Deliverables

- `rules.md` with sections: `## Overview`, `## Setup`,
  `## Turn structure`, `## Winning`, `## Example round` (a concrete
  play-through of at least one full round with named players and
  specific rolls/values).

## Constraints

- At most 800 words. The rules must be complete: a reader with a pair
  of dice should never face a situation the rules do not cover.
