## Task

Design a two-player game using ONLY a standard double-six domino set (28
tiles, nothing printed, drawn, or added). It must involve meaningful
decisions — not pure luck — and a match should take 10–20 minutes.

## Deliverables

- `rules.md` with sections: `## Overview`, `## Setup`,
  `## Turn structure`, `## Winning`, `## Example round` (a concrete
  play-through of at least one full round with named players and specific
  tiles).

## Constraints

- At most 800 words. The rules must be complete: a reader with a domino
  set should never face a situation the rules do not cover.
