## Task

Design a two-player game using ONLY a standard double-six domino set —
the 28 tiles, and nothing else (no dice, no counters, no pen and paper,
nothing printed or added). It must involve meaningful decisions — not
pure luck — and a match should take 10–20 minutes.

## Deliverables

- `rules.md` with sections: `## Overview`, `## Setup`,
  `## Turn structure`, `## Winning`, `## Example round` (a concrete
  play-through of at least one full round with named players and
  specific tiles).

## Constraints

- At most 800 words. The rules must be complete: a reader with a domino
  set should never face a situation the rules do not cover.
