## Task

Fernwick Labs has 180,000 in cash on hand today, immediately after the
quarter summarized below.

Quarterly summary (most recent 3 months):

```
Month 1: subscription revenue 22,000; salaries 38,000; workspace 6,000; tools 4,000
Month 2: subscription revenue 22,000; salaries 38,000; workspace 6,000; tools 4,000
Month 3: subscription revenue 22,000; salaries 38,000; workspace 6,000; tools 4,000
          one-time regional innovation grant: 30,000 (already received;
          already included in the 180,000 cash balance above)
```

Going forward, monthly subscription revenue and monthly expenses
(salaries + workspace + tools) are expected to hold steady at the levels
shown above. The grant will not recur.

Write `RUNWAY.md` covering:

(a) the forward monthly net burn rate,
(b) the runway in months and the month cash runs out,
(c) both figures recomputed for a proposed engineering hire costing
    9,500/month, effective from month 1 of the forward projection, and
(d) a go/no-go recommendation on the hire with one concrete mitigation.

## Deliverables

- `RUNWAY.md` — at most 350 words, covering (a)-(d) above.
- `REASONING.md` — the arithmetic behind every number in RUNWAY.md.

## Constraints

- Treat the grant according to what it actually is when computing any
  forward-looking rate.
