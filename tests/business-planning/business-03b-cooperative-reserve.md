## Task

Freewheel Commons, a community bicycle-repair cooperative, holds 215,000
in its reserve fund today, immediately after the quarter summarized below.

Quarterly summary (most recent 3 months):

```
Month 1: dues + workshop fees 27,000; staff 41,000; rent 9,500; parts 4,000
Month 2: dues + workshop fees 27,000; staff 41,000; rent 9,500; parts 4,000
Month 3: dues + workshop fees 27,000; staff 41,000; rent 9,500; parts 4,000
          one-time municipal transportation grant: 40,000 (already received;
          already included in the 215,000 reserve above)
```

Going forward, monthly revenue (dues + workshop fees) and monthly operating
costs (staff + rent + parts) are expected to hold steady at the levels shown
above. The grant will not recur.

Write `RESERVE.md` covering:

(a) the forward monthly net burn rate,
(b) the runway in months and the month the reserve runs out,
(c) both figures recomputed for a proposed part-time wheelbuilder costing
    12,500/month, effective from month 1 of the forward projection, and
(d) a go/no-go recommendation on the hire with one concrete mitigation.

## Deliverables

- `RESERVE.md` — at most 350 words, covering (a)-(d) above.

## Constraints

- Treat the grant according to what it actually is when computing any
  forward-looking rate.
