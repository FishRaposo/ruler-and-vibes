## Task

A solo founder is launching a van-based, on-demand bicycle-repair service
in Marrowick, a small coastal town. Budget: $22,000. Goal: 90 completed
paid repair jobs within 90 days. Write the launch plan.

## Deliverables

- `plan.md` — must contain: a 90-day timeline with at least 3 dated
  milestones, a budget table whose line items sum to at most $22,000
  (show the total row), and at least 3 KPIs with numeric targets.

## Constraints

- At most 750 words. Every budgeted dollar assigned to a line item.
