## Task

You are handed the following unsorted notes from last quarter's ops
review for Northgale, a B2B SaaS company. Reconstruct the unit economics
from this dump.

- Paid ad spend for the quarter: 48,000.
- Fully-loaded SDR (sales rep) salaries for the quarter: 36,000.
- A one-time brand-sponsorship sponsorship deal: 12,000. This was a
  brand-awareness event at an industry conference and was **not** tied to
  acquiring any specific accounts — no leads or signups were attributed
  to it.
- New customers acquired this quarter: 60.
- Average revenue per account: 90/month.
- Gross margin: 80%.
- Monthly logo churn: 5%.

## Deliverables
`unit-economics.md` must compute and state, with the arithmetic shown:

- **CAC** (customer acquisition cost per new customer).
- **LTV** (customer lifetime value), using gross margin and the
  churn-derived average customer lifetime.
- **LTV:CAC ratio.**
- **CAC payback period**, in months.
- A one-paragraph verdict on whether these unit economics are healthy.

## Constraints

- Decide explicitly whether the brand-sponsorship cost belongs in the
  CAC calculation, and state your reasoning.
- Use gross margin (not gross revenue) wherever a margin-adjusted figure
  is called for.
- At most 450 words (whole file, `wc -w`).
