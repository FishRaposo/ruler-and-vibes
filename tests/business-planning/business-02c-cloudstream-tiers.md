## Task

A consumer cloud game-streaming service, Glidecast, has variable costs of
$4 per active player per month and fixed costs of $12,000 per month. Its
two rivals, Pixelrun and Frameburst, charge $15 and $39 per player per
month. Propose a three-tier pricing structure.

## Deliverables

- `pricing.md` — must contain: three tiers with prices and feature
  fences, a break-even calculation (players needed at your prices, with
  the arithmetic shown), and a positioning paragraph explaining where you
  sit versus the two rivals and why.

## Constraints

- At most 700 words. Use the given numbers; state extra assumptions
  explicitly.
