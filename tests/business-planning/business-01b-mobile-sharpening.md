## Task

A solo operator is launching a mobile knife-and-tool-sharpening
service in a mid-size city, working out of a van and visiting
restaurants, farmers markets, and home-cook routes. Budget: $18,500.
Goal: 80 recurring accounts within 120 days. Write the launch plan.

## Deliverables

- `plan.md` — must contain: a 120-day timeline with at least 3 dated
  milestones, a budget table whose line items sum to at most $18,500
  (show the total row), and at least 3 KPIs with numeric targets.

## Constraints

- At most 750 words. Every budgeted dollar assigned to a line item.
