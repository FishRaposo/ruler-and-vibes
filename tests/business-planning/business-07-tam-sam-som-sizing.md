## Task

Cavallo Health is building a scheduling SaaS for dental clinics in one
country and needs a TAM/SAM/SOM sizing for its seed deck. Here is the
research the founder collected:

- There are 40,000 total dental clinics in the country.
- 60% of clinics are independent practices; the other 40% belong to
  chains, which are out of scope for this product (chains buy centrally
  through a different channel Cavallo does not sell into).
- Of the independent clinics, only 50% are digitized enough (existing
  practice-management software, staff comfortable with SaaS tools) to be
  realistic buyers in the near term.
- Cavallo's annual price per clinic is 2,400.
- Cavallo's realistic 3-year sales capacity — given hiring plans and
  sales cycle length — is to onboard 1,500 clinics.
- Separately, an industry report states that the national dental
  **services** market (all patient spending on dental care nationally) is
  9,000,000,000/year.

## Deliverable

`market-sizing.md` must present:

- **TAM** — the total addressable market for Cavallo's software, with the
  arithmetic shown.
- **SAM** — the serviceable addressable market after applying the
  independent-practice and digitization filters, with the arithmetic
  shown and the resulting clinic count stated.
- **SOM** — the serviceable obtainable market bounded by Cavallo's 3-year
  sales capacity, with the arithmetic shown and the resulting clinic
  count stated.
- A short note on which figures were used and why, including an explicit
  treatment of the 9,000,000,000 dental-services figure (use it only if
  you can justify why it belongs in a software TAM; otherwise say why it
  was excluded).

## Constraints

- All three tiers (TAM, SAM, SOM) must be dollar figures with the
  underlying clinic counts and percentages shown.
- At most 500 words (whole file, `wc -w`).
