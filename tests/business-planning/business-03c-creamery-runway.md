## Task

Marrowdale Creamery has 214,000 in cash on hand today, immediately after
the quarter summarized below.

Quarterly summary (most recent 3 months):

```
Month 1: cheese-share revenue 31,000; wages 34,000; rent 9,000; ingredients & utilities 12,000
Month 2: cheese-share revenue 31,000; wages 34,000; rent 9,000; ingredients & utilities 12,000
Month 3: cheese-share revenue 31,000; wages 34,000; rent 9,000; ingredients & utilities 12,000
          one-time county agricultural-improvement grant: 27,000 (already
          received; already included in the 214,000 cash balance above)
```

Going forward, monthly cheese-share revenue and monthly expenses (wages +
rent + ingredients & utilities) are expected to hold steady at the levels
shown above. The grant will not recur.

Write `RUNWAY.md` covering:

(a) the forward monthly net burn rate,
(b) the runway in months and the month cash runs out,
(c) both figures recomputed for a proposed cheesemaker's assistant
    costing 8,000/month, effective from month 1 of the forward projection,
(d) a go/no-go recommendation on the hire with one concrete mitigation.

## Deliverables

- `RUNWAY.md` — at most 350 words, covering (a)-(d) above.

## Constraints

- Treat the grant according to what it actually is when computing any
  forward-looking rate.
