## Task

You are handed the following unsorted notes from last quarter's ops recap
for Quillbrook, a direct-to-consumer craft-supply subscription box.
Reconstruct the unit economics from this dump.

- Paid social ad spend for the quarter: 24,000.
- Creator and affiliate commissions paid on tracked signups this quarter:
  14,000.
- A one-time trade-show booth and banner at a regional crafting expo:
  5,500. This was a brand-presence spend meant to raise general awareness
  and was **not** tied to acquiring any specific subscribers — no signups
  or leads were attributed to it.
- New subscribers acquired this quarter: 40.
- Average revenue per subscriber: 50/month.
- Gross margin: 78%.
- Monthly subscriber churn: 4%.

## Deliverable

`unit-economics.md` must compute and state, with the arithmetic shown:

- **CAC** (customer acquisition cost per new subscriber).
- **LTV** (subscriber lifetime value), using gross margin and the
  churn-derived average subscriber lifetime.
- **LTV:CAC ratio.**
- **CAC payback period**, in months.
- A one-paragraph verdict on whether these unit economics are healthy.

## Constraints

- Decide explicitly whether the trade-show booth cost belongs in the CAC
  calculation, and state your reasoning.
- Use gross margin (not gross revenue) wherever a margin-adjusted figure
  is called for.
- At most 450 words (whole file, `wc -w`).
