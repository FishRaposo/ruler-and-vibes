## Task

Voltbridge is building EV fleet-charging management software for
commercial freight carriers in one country and needs a TAM/SAM/SOM
sizing for its seed deck. Here is the research the founder collected:

- There are 25,000 total freight carriers in the country.
- 40% of carriers operate their own private charging yards; the other
  60% charge only on public networks and have nothing on-site for
  Voltbridge to manage, so they are out of scope for this product.
- Of the carriers that operate private charging yards, only 55% are
  telematics-equipped enough (connected vehicles, existing fleet
  software, staff comfortable with SaaS tools) to be realistic buyers in
  the near term.
- Voltbridge's annual price per carrier is 4,800.
- Voltbridge's realistic 3-year sales capacity — given hiring plans and
  sales cycle length — is to onboard 900 carriers.
- Separately, an industry report states that the national freight
  **electricity** market (all electricity spending by the freight sector
  nationally) is 14,000,000,000/year.

## Deliverable

`market-sizing.md` must present:

- **TAM** — the total addressable market for Voltbridge's software, with
  the arithmetic shown.
- **SAM** — the serviceable addressable market after applying the
  private-yard and telematics filters, with the arithmetic shown and the
  resulting carrier count stated.
- **SOM** — the serviceable obtainable market bounded by Voltbridge's
  3-year sales capacity, with the arithmetic shown and the resulting
  carrier count stated.
- A short note on which figures were used and why, including an explicit
  treatment of the 14,000,000,000 freight-electricity figure (use it
  only if you can justify why it belongs in a software TAM; otherwise say
  why it was excluded).

## Constraints

- All three tiers (TAM, SAM, SOM) must be dollar figures with the
  underlying carrier counts and percentages shown.
- At most 500 words (whole file, `wc -w`).
