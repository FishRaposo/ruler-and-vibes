## Task

Auricle Systems is building hearing-aid fitting and tele-audiology
software for independent audiology clinics in one country and needs a
TAM/SAM/SOM sizing for its seed deck. Here is the research the founder
collected:

- There are 32,000 total independent audiology clinics in the country.
- 45% of these clinics run their own on-site fitting and repair room;
  the other 55% refer all fitting work out to a franchise network that
  handles it centrally through a separate channel Auricle does not sell
  into, so they are out of scope for this product.
- Of the clinics that run their own fitting room, only 65% are
  cloud-connected enough (existing electronic-records system, staff
  comfortable with SaaS tools) to be realistic buyers in the near term.
- Auricle's annual price per clinic is 3,600.
- Auricle's realistic 3-year sales capacity — given hiring plans and
  sales cycle length — is to onboard 1,300 clinics.
- Separately, an industry report states that the national hearing-care
  **services** market (all patient spending on exams, devices, and
  follow-up care nationally) is 11,000,000,000/year.

## Deliverables
`market-sizing.md` must present:

- **TAM** — the total addressable market for Auricle's software, with
  the arithmetic shown.
- **SAM** — the serviceable addressable market after applying the
  own-fitting-room and cloud-connected filters, with the arithmetic
  shown and the resulting clinic count stated.
- **SOM** — the serviceable obtainable market bounded by Auricle's
  3-year sales capacity, with the arithmetic shown and the resulting
  clinic count stated.
- A short note on which figures were used and why, including an explicit
  treatment of the 11,000,000,000 hearing-care-services figure (use it
  only if you can justify why it belongs in a software TAM; otherwise say
  why it was excluded).

## Constraints

- All three tiers (TAM, SAM, SOM) must be dollar figures with the
  underlying clinic counts and percentages shown.
- At most 500 words (whole file, `wc -w`).
