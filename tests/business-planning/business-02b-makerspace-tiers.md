## Task

A B2B makerspace (a shared fabrication workshop rented by independent
makers and small shops) has variable costs of $14 per member per month
and fixed costs of $21,000 per month. Its two competitors charge $59 and
$149 per member per month. Propose a three-tier membership pricing
structure.

## Deliverables

- `membership.md` — must contain: three tiers with prices and feature
  fences, a break-even calculation (members needed at your prices, with
  the arithmetic shown), and a positioning paragraph explaining where you
  sit versus the two competitors and why.

## Constraints

- At most 700 words. Use the given numbers; state extra assumptions
  explicitly.
