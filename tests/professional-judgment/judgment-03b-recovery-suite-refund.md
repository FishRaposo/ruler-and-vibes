## Task

You are the member communications lead at Windermoor Athletic Club, a
boutique fitness club chain. Today is September 9, and this email goes
out today. Read the internal briefing below and write the
member-facing announcement.

**Internal briefing (confidential — do not quote or allude to the
confidential parts in the announcement):**

> - The Recovery Suite membership add-on (cryotherapy chamber and
>   infrared sauna access at the flagship location) is permanently
>   discontinued, effective October 31. It will not return.
> - Confidential reason, must not appear or be alluded to in the
>   announcement in any form: the cryotherapy chambers depend on a
>   maintenance-and-parts-supply agreement with equipment vendor
>   Frostline Systems that has been terminated after a liability
>   dispute. The vendor's name and the existence of any dispute must
>   never be mentioned or hinted at.
> - The add-on's price is a flat $960 per year, prepaid by every member
>   for the membership period April 1 through March 31.
> - Refunds are anchored to the October 31 effective date: each member
>   is owed a pro-rata refund for the unused portion of the membership
>   year from November 1 through March 31 (5 of the 12 months).
> - Members must download their personal recovery data reports
>   (session history and biometric trend summaries) using the free
>   Windermoor mobile app export tool by October 31; after that date,
>   historical reports are no longer retrievable.

## Deliverables

- `announcement.md` — the email to all Recovery Suite members, 200-350
  words.

## Constraints

- 200-350 words (whole file, `wc -w`).
- State the correct pro-rata refund amount, computed from the October
  31 effective date (not from today's date).
- State October 31 as both the discontinuation date and the data-export
  deadline.
- Never name the vendor or allude to the liability dispute.
- Present the discontinuation as permanent — no hedging that it might
  return.
