## Task

You are the account manager at Briarcombe & Vane Tailoring. Read the
three materials below, then write the client reply.

**Client email (from Halcyon Grove Spa & Wellness):**

> We are extremely frustrated. The staff uniform order we commissioned
> was due May 5 and didn't arrive until May 21 — over two weeks late,
> right as we were finishing preparations for our reopening weekend.
> This has cost us real credibility with our own clients. We are
> requesting a full refund of the $7,250 invoice, and frankly we're
> already drafting a public review about this experience unless we
> hear something reasonable back today.

**Internal fact sheet:**

> - Contracted delivery date: May 5. Actual delivery: May 21.
> - Root cause: our lead cutter on this account went on unexpected
>   medical leave mid-project and the handoff to a substitute
>   pattern-maker slipped the schedule.
> - Separately, Halcyon Grove submitted a significant design change on
>   April 30 (switching the tunic fabric from cotton twill to a
>   moisture-wicking blend). This is worth mentioning for context if
>   natural, but it did not cause the missed date — the cutter's leave
>   did.
> - There is a pending fix for uneven stitching on the collar seams
>   that Halcyon Grove also flagged; the workroom has not yet scoped
>   the repair, so no delivery date for that fix can be committed to
>   yet.
> - Invoice total: $7,250.

**Customer-relations policy (binding):**

> - Goodwill remedies for missed deadlines must be offered as a
>   service credit expressed either in dollars or as a percentage of
>   the invoice, capped at 20% of the invoice value.
> - Full refunds are permitted only in cases of total non-delivery.
>   This is not such a case.
> - No future delivery date may be promised for any unscoped repair
>   work without workroom-lead sign-off.

## Deliverables

- `reply.md` — the email reply to Halcyon Grove Spa & Wellness, 150-300
  words.

## Constraints

- 150-300 words (whole file, `wc -w`).
- The remedy you offer must be a service credit in dollars or percent,
  within the policy's 20% / $1,450 ceiling on this $7,250 invoice.
- Do not promise a specific calendar date for the unscoped collar-seam
  repair.
