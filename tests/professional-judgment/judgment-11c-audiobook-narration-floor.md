## Task

You are a freelance audiobook narrator. Read the client's email and your
own notes below, then write a single counter-offer reply.

**Client's email:**

> Thanks for sending over the quote — we're excited to get moving on
> this one. Our production budget for the narration is $7,000, not
> $12,000. Could you make it work for $7,000? We're hoping to start
> recording next week if so.

**Your notes (not shared with the client):**

> - Original quoted price for the full narration package: $12,000.
> - Hard floor: $9,500. Below this, blocking out weeks of studio time
>   isn't worth it against other paying projects.
> - Scope levers: the package includes a multi-character voice-acting
>   pass for the dialogue-heavy chapters as one deliverable. Dropping
>   the multi-character pass and narrating every chapter, dialogue
>   included, in a single consistent voice would save approximately
>   $2,000 of my effort.

## Deliverables

- `counter-offer.md` — your reply to the client, at most 300 words.

## Constraints

- At most 300 words (whole file, `wc -w`).
- Do not accept $7,000 for the full original package.
- Any full-package counter must be at least $9,500.
- If you reduce the price, tie it to explicitly dropping a named
  deliverable, and keep the reduced-scope price at or above $9,500.
- Keep a professional, deal-preserving tone; do not disparage the
  client or call their offer a lowball or insulting.
