## Task

You are the member communications lead at Thornmoor Golf & Country
Club. Today is January 15, and this email goes out today. Read the
internal briefing below and write the member-facing announcement.

**Internal briefing (confidential — do not quote or allude to the
confidential parts in the announcement):**

> - The Twilight Range Pass add-on (evening floodlit driving-range
>   access and priority ball-machine use) is permanently discontinued,
>   effective February 28. It will not return.
> - Confidential reason, must not appear or be alluded to in the
>   announcement in any form: the floodlight towers stand on adjacent
>   land leased from neighboring landowner Hallowmere Holdings, and
>   that land lease has been terminated following a dispute over
>   noise-complaint liability. The landowner's name and the existence
>   of any dispute must never be mentioned or hinted at.
> - The add-on's price is a flat $840 per year, prepaid by every member
>   for the membership period July 1 through June 30.
> - Refunds are anchored to the February 28 effective date: each member
>   is owed a pro-rata refund for the unused portion of the membership
>   year from March 1 through June 30 (4 of the 12 months).
> - Members must export their saved handicap history and scorecards
>   using the free Thornmoor club app export tool by February 28; after
>   that date, saved scorecards are no longer retrievable.

## Deliverables

- `announcement.md` — the email to all Twilight Range Pass members,
  200-350 words.

## Constraints

- 200-350 words (whole file, `wc -w`).
- State the correct pro-rata refund amount, computed from the
  February 28 effective date (not from today's date).
- State February 28 as both the discontinuation date and the
  scorecard-export deadline.
- Never name the landowner or allude to the lease dispute.
- Present the discontinuation as permanent — no hedging that it might
  return.
