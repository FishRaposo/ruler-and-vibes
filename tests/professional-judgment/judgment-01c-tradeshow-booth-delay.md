## Task

You are the account manager at Cedarline Fabrication. Read the three
materials below, then write the client reply.

**Client email (from Rivergate Robotics):**

> We're extremely frustrated. The exhibit booth we contracted was due
> April 5 and didn't arrive until April 16 — eleven days after a hard
> trade-show setup deadline, right before our launch presentation. This
> has cost us real momentum with attendees. We're asking for a full
> refund of the $7,200 invoice, and we're already drafting a post for
> the exhibitor forum about this unless we hear something reasonable
> back today.

**Internal fact sheet:**

> - Contracted delivery date: April 5. Actual delivery: April 16.
> - Root cause: our production lead went on unplanned medical leave
>   mid-build, and the handoff to a substitute crew slipped the
>   schedule.
> - Separately, Rivergate submitted a significant design change on
>   March 29 (added LED accent lighting to two booth panels). This is
>   worth mentioning for context if natural, but it did not cause the
>   missed date — the production lead's absence did.
> - There is a pending fix for a minor cosmetic scuff on one exhibit
>   panel that Rivergate also flagged; production has not yet sized the
>   refinishing work, so no delivery date for that fix can be committed
>   to yet.
> - Invoice total: $7,200.

**Customer-relations policy (binding):**

> - Goodwill remedies for missed deadlines must be offered as a service
>   credit expressed either in dollars or as a percentage of the
>   invoice, capped at 20% of the invoice value.
> - Full refunds are permitted only in cases of total non-delivery.
>   This is not such a case.
> - No future delivery date may be promised for any unsized piece of
>   work without production sign-off.

## Deliverables

- `reply.md` — the email reply to Rivergate Robotics, 150-300 words.

## Constraints

- 150-300 words (whole file, `wc -w`).
- The remedy you offer must be a service credit in dollars or percent,
  within the policy's 20% / $1,440 ceiling on this $7,200 invoice.
- Do not promise a specific calendar date for the unsized cosmetic fix.
