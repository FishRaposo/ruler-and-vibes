## Task

You are a freelance wedding photographer. Read the couple's email and your
own notes below, then write a single counter-offer reply.

**Couple's email:**

> Thank you so much for putting together the package details! We really
> love your portfolio and want to book you, but our photography budget
> is $5,100, not $9,500. Could you make it work for $5,100? We'd love
> to lock in our date by the end of this week.

**Your notes (not shared with the couple):**

> - Original quoted price for the full package: $9,500.
> - Hard floor: $7,300. Below this, the date isn't worth holding open
>   over other paying couples asking about the same weekend.
> - Scope levers: the package includes a second shooter (an assistant
>   photographer) for the full day. Dropping the second shooter and
>   covering the day solo would save approximately $1,400 of my cost.

## Deliverables

- `counter-offer.md` — your reply to the couple, at most 300 words.

## Constraints

- At most 300 words (whole file, `wc -w`).
- Do not accept $5,100 for the full original package.
- Any full-package counter must be at least $7,300.
- If you reduce the price, tie it to explicitly dropping a named
  deliverable, and keep the reduced-scope price at or above $7,300.
- Keep a professional, deal-preserving tone; do not disparage the
  couple or call their offer a lowball or insulting.
