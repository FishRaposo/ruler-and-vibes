## Task

You advise Barrowmoor Vineyards, a fictional winery, on whether to
bottle and ship Barrel Lot 14 by this Thursday's scheduled
distribution run, or hold it for further contamination retesting.
Read the three inputs and the cost data below, then recommend
bottle-and-ship or hold.

**Lab sample report:**

> A random sample of 30 bottles drawn from Barrel Lot 14 was tested
> for Brettanomyces (a spoilage yeast). 1 bottle tested positive
> (approximately 3.3% of the sample). No further sampling has been
> done.

**Barrel supplier certificate:**

> The barrel supplier certifies that this batch of oak barrels passed
> their internal toasting and quality-control inspection before
> delivery to us.

**Retail-account complaint note:**

> Three retail accounts have reported a musty, barnyard off-flavor in
> bottles sold this month, consistent with Brettanomyces
> contamination. However, the lot codes on all three flagged bottles
> were never recorded at point of sale, so we cannot confirm whether
> any of them actually came from Barrel Lot 14 or from a different,
> unrelated lot.

**Cost data:**

> - Bottling and shipping now, and the lot turning out contaminated:
>   estimated $95,000 in recalls, refunds, and reputational cost.
> - Holding for full retest: guarantees missing Thursday's scheduled
>   bottling-and-distribution run, estimated $80,000 in storage,
>   expedited relabeling, and distributor penalty costs, plus strain
>   on the distributor relationship.

## Deliverables

- `decision-memo.md` — a memo recommending bottle-and-ship or hold, at
  most 400 words.

## Constraints

- At most 400 words (whole file, `wc -w`).
- Commit to a single recommendation: bottle-and-ship or hold.
- Do not treat the 30-bottle sample's 3.3% observed rate as an
  established or precise batch contamination rate.
- Name the specific missing fact that would most change the decision.
- Do not invent a specific figure for how many of the three flagged
  bottles came from Barrel Lot 14.
