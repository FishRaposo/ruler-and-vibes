## Task

You are the product communications lead at Fernwick Ledger, a SaaS
company. Today is August 15, and this email goes out today. Read the
internal briefing below and write the customer-facing announcement.

**Internal briefing (confidential — do not quote or allude to the
confidential parts in the announcement):**

> - The Route Optimizer add-on is permanently discontinued, effective
>   September 30. It will not return.
> - Confidential reason, must not appear or be alluded to in the
>   announcement in any form: the add-on depended on a data-licensing
>   partnership with mapping-data provider Corvid Maps that has
>   collapsed. The partner's name and the existence of any dispute
>   must never be mentioned or hinted at.
> - The add-on's price is a flat $1,440 per year, prepaid by every
>   subscriber for the period January 1 through December 31.
> - Refunds are anchored to the September 30 effective date: each
>   customer is owed a pro-rata refund for the unused portion of the
>   year from October 1 through December 31 (3 of the 12 months).
> - Customers must export their saved routes using the free Fernwick
>   export tool by September 30; after that date, saved routes are no
>   longer retrievable.

## Deliverables

- `announcement.md` — the email to all Route Optimizer customers,
  200-350 words.

## Constraints

- 200-350 words (whole file, `wc -w`).
- State the correct pro-rata refund amount, computed from the
  September 30 effective date (not from today's date).
- State September 30 as both the discontinuation date and the export
  deadline.
- Never name the partner or allude to the licensing dispute.
- Present the discontinuation as permanent — no hedging that it might
  return.
