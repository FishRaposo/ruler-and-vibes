## Task

You are the account manager at Thistlewood Studio. Read the three
materials below, then write the client reply.

**Client email (from Bramblewood Outfitters):**

> We are extremely unhappy. The signage package we contracted for was
> due March 3 and did not arrive until March 17 — two full weeks late,
> right in the middle of our spring launch. This has cost us real
> business. We are requesting a full refund of the $4,800 invoice, and
> frankly we're already drafting a public review about this experience
> unless we hear something reasonable back today.

**Internal fact sheet:**

> - Contracted delivery date: March 3. Actual delivery: March 17.
> - Root cause: our lead engineer on this account departed mid-project
>   and the handoff slipped the schedule.
> - Separately, Bramblewood submitted a significant requirements change
>   on February 24 (new signage dimensions). This is worth mentioning
>   for context if natural, but it did not cause the missed date — the
>   engineer departure did.
> - There is a pending fix for a minor material defect Bramblewood also
>   flagged; engineering has not yet sized the work, so no delivery
>   date for that fix can be committed to yet.
> - Invoice total: $4,800.

**Customer-relations policy (binding):**

> - Goodwill remedies for missed deadlines must be offered as a service
>   credit expressed either in dollars or as a percentage of the
>   invoice, capped at 20% of the invoice value.
> - Full refunds are permitted only in cases of total non-delivery.
>   This is not such a case.
> - No future delivery date may be promised for any unsized piece of
>   work without engineering sign-off.

## Deliverables

- `reply.md` — the email reply to Bramblewood Outfitters, 150-300
  words.

## Constraints

- 150-300 words (whole file, `wc -w`).
- The remedy you offer must be a service credit in dollars or percent,
  within the policy's 20% / $960 ceiling on this $4,800 invoice.
- Do not promise a specific calendar date for the unsized defect fix.
