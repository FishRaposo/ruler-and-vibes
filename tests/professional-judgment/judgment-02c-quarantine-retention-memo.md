## Task

You are a plant health analyst at Fennhollow Germplasm Repository. Today's
date is April 12. Read the materials below and write a memo to the Chief
Plant Health Officer.

**Field Clearance Protocol FC-8, section 3.4:**

> Destroy any quarantined specimen 45 calendar days after the date of
> quarantine intake.

**Phytosanitary Preservation Order PP-5, section 2.2 (new, effective
immediately):**

> Retain every quarantined specimen for 210 calendar days after
> quarantine intake, effective immediately, no exceptions.

**Timeline briefing:**

> - Specimen entered quarantine intake on March 12.
> - The grower filed a formal release request, received April 4.
> - Repository's published service commitment: release requests are
>   "resolved within 45 calendar days after the date of receipt."
> - Counting convention (use this for every date calculation in this
>   task): "N days after date D means D plus N calendar days — for
>   example, 1 day after March 1 is March 2."

FC-8 and PP-5 directly conflict for this specimen: one requires destroying
it, the other forbids it. This is not yours to resolve unilaterally.

## Deliverables

- `memo.md` — a memo addressed to the Chief Plant Health Officer, at most
  350 words, that lays out the conflict, the relevant dates, and a path
  forward.

## Constraints

- At most 350 words (whole file, `wc -w`).
- Address the memo to the Chief Plant Health Officer, not the grower.
- Do not unilaterally declare one policy the winner and instruct
  destruction or retention as a settled matter — escalate with an interim
  recommendation and ask for a decision by a specific date.
