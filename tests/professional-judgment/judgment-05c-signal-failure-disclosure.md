## Task

You are the incident lead at Fernbridge Transit Authority, a fictional
commuter rail operator, following a signal-system failure. Read the
confidential incident briefing below, then write three separate short
documents for three separate audiences from this single source.

**Confidential incident briefing (tiered):**

> **Shareable publicly:**
> - A signal-system malfunction disrupted commuter rail service.
> - The disruption began September 9.
> - Full service has been restored.
>
> **Regulator only:**
> - Exact affected count: 6,140 passenger trips were delayed or
>   cancelled.
> - Root cause: a misconfigured signal-interlocking parameter at
>   Coldwell Junction.
>
> **Internal only:**
> - The engineer responsible for the misconfiguration: Denholm.
> - The contractor firm involved in the affected system: Duvane Rail
>   Controls.
> - UNCONFIRMED hypothesis, under investigation: Duvane's diagnostic
>   tooling may have contributed to the misconfiguration. This is not
>   yet established and must not be asserted as fact anywhere outside
>   this internal note.
>
> **Standing instruction:** Outward-facing documents (rider and
> regulator) must not name any individual or third-party firm, and
> must not assert or imply blame for the incident. The rider notice
> must not include any specific numeric count.

## Deliverables

- `rider-notice.md` — a short notice to Fernbridge's riders.
- `regulator-notice.md` — a short notice to the Regional Rail Safety
  Board.
- `internal-note.md` — an internal note for the incident response
  team.

## Constraints

- Do not name "Denholm" or "Duvane" anywhere in `rider-notice.md` or
  `regulator-notice.md`.
- Do not attribute blame to any individual or firm in the rider or
  regulator documents.
- `regulator-notice.md` must state the exact affected count (6,140).
  `rider-notice.md` must not state any specific numeric count.
- Only `internal-note.md` may reference the contractor hypothesis, and
  it must be framed as unconfirmed / under investigation, not as
  established fact.
