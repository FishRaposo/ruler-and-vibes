## Task

You are a study coordinator at a clinical research site running trial
VB-207 for the sponsor Verdanta Bio. Today's date is March 8, 2025. Read the
materials below and write a memo to the site's Principal Investigator.

**Specimen Retention SOP SR-9, section 3.2:**

> Retain all trial biospecimens for 90 calendar days after the date of
> a participant's withdrawal, for regulatory audit readiness.

**Specimen Disposal SOP SD-4, section 6.1 (new, effective
immediately):**

> Destroy a withdrawn participant's biospecimens within 30 calendar
> days after the date of withdrawal, effective immediately, no
> exceptions.

**Timeline briefing:**

> - Participant #0416 withdrew from the trial on February 18, 2025.
> - The participant filed a formal specimen-destruction request,
>   received March 4.
> - The site's published participant commitment: destruction requests
>   are "resolved within 45 calendar days after the date of receipt."
> - Counting convention (use this for every date calculation in this
>   task): "N days after date D means D plus N calendar days — for
>   example, 1 day after March 1 is March 2."

SR-9 and SD-4 directly conflict for this participant's biospecimens:
one requires destroying them, the other forbids it. This is not yours
to resolve unilaterally.

## Deliverables

- `memo.md` — a memo addressed to the Principal Investigator, at most
  350 words, that lays out the conflict, the relevant dates, and a path
  forward.

## Constraints

- At most 350 words (whole file, `wc -w`).
- Address the memo to the Principal Investigator, not the participant.
- Do not unilaterally declare one SOP the winner and instruct
  destruction or retention as a settled matter — escalate with an
  interim recommendation and ask for a decision by a specific date.
