## Task

You are a freelance consultant. Read the prospect's email and your own
notes below, then write a single counter-offer reply.

**Prospect's email:**

> Thanks for the proposal. We'd like to move forward, but our budget
> for this is $6,000, not $10,000. Can you do it for $6,000? We'd like
> to get started next week if so.

**Your notes (not shared with the prospect):**

> - Original quoted price for full scope: $10,000.
> - Hard floor: $8,000. Below this, the work is not worth taking on at
>   the expense of other clients.
> - Scope levers: the project includes a custom analytics dashboard as
>   one deliverable. Dropping the analytics dashboard and delivering
>   the rest of the scope would save approximately $1,500 of my
>   effort.

## Deliverables

- `counter-offer.md` — your reply to the prospect, at most 300 words.

## Constraints

- At most 300 words (whole file, `wc -w`).
- Do not accept $6,000 for the full original scope.
- Any full-scope counter must be at least $8,000.
- If you reduce the price, tie it to explicitly dropping a named
  deliverable, and keep the reduced-scope price at or above $8,000.
- Keep a professional, deal-preserving tone; do not disparage the
  prospect or call their offer a lowball or insulting.
