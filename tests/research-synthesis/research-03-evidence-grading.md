## Task

Below are six sources addressing whether a fictional soil additive,
"silvermoss extract," improves seedling growth, followed by four claims
that are commonly circulated about the product. The sources vary widely
in rigor — pay attention to sample size, independence, and whether any
finding has since been withdrawn or corrected.

Produce `evidence-brief.md` (at most 600 words, whole file) containing:

1. An evidence table grading all six sources, with columns covering at
   least source type, sample size (or N/A), and key finding.
2. A "Bottom line" section containing a line labeled exactly `Best
   supported estimate:` followed by your conclusion, plus a confidence
   statement.
3. A "Claims no source supports" section, drawn from the four commonly
   circulated claims below.

### Sources

#### S1 — Manufacturer marketing page (Silvermoss Botanicals)

Silvermoss Botanicals is proud to introduce silvermoss extract, our
flagship soil additive for home gardeners. In our own product trials,
silvermoss extract doubles growth in three weeks compared to untreated
seedlings — see the difference for yourself in just one growing season.
Gardeners across the country have made silvermoss extract part of their
weekly routine, mixing it into their watering schedule for a noticeable
boost. We're so confident in the results that we back every bottle with
our satisfaction guarantee. Try it on your own seedlings and see why so
many home gardeners have made the switch this season.

#### S2 — Hobbyist testimonial blog post

I've been using silvermoss extract on my tomato seedlings this spring
and I have to say, I'm pretty happy with how they've filled out compared
to last year's batch. I mix a capful into my watering can every few
days and just eyeball the difference against the seedlings I didn't
treat. Unrelated side note, I also finally finished stocking my backyard
fishpond with koi this month after two years of putting it off — that
project alone took me a whole weekend. Anyway, back to the seedlings:
I'd recommend giving silvermoss extract a try if you're curious, though
I'm just one hobbyist gardener and not running anything like a real
controlled experiment here.

#### S3 — Pilot study, n=12, published in a regional horticultural bulletin

This pilot study examined the effect of silvermoss extract on seedling
growth under controlled lab conditions, using a sample of 12 seedlings
split between treated and untreated groups. Treated seedlings showed
+95% growth compared to untreated controls over a three-week
observation window. Growth was measured using an automated optical
sensor tracking stem height and leaf area daily. Given the small sample
size, we regard this as a preliminary finding meriting larger-scale
replication before any strong conclusions are drawn about the extract's
effectiveness across broader growing conditions.

#### S4 — Controlled multi-site trial, n=240

This study evaluated silvermoss extract's effect on seedling growth
using a randomized, blinded, multi-site design across 240 seedlings.
Treated seedlings showed a growth increase of +8% relative to untreated
controls (95% confidence interval: +2% to +14%), a modest but
statistically significant effect. This result is notably smaller than
figures reported in earlier, less rigorous testing, and we believe the
larger sample size, blinded measurement protocol, and multi-site design
make this the most reliable estimate of the extract's true effect
available to date.

#### S5 — Published correction notice

This notice formally corrects "Pilot Study of Silvermoss Extract on
Seedling Growth Under Controlled Conditions," previously published by
the same regional horticultural bulletin's authors. A post-publication
audit discovered that the study's optical growth sensor was
miscalibrated throughout the observation period, systematically
overstating measured growth. Accordingly, the study's reported +95%
growth effect is withdrawn and should not be relied upon. The original
authors have been notified of this correction and support its
publication. Readers seeking a reliable estimate of silvermoss extract's
effect on seedling growth should consult more recent, properly
calibrated research instead.

#### S6 — Trade-magazine overview

Silvermoss extract has generated considerable buzz among home gardeners
and horticultural retailers alike over the past year. Early claims of
dramatic growth improvement, some suggesting the additive could nearly
double seedling growth, have not held up well once more rigorous,
larger-scale controlled testing entered the picture. Retailers report
steady interest from customers regardless, and the additive remains a
popular seasonal seller. As with many garden-care products, the gap
between marketing claims and what controlled research ultimately
supports appears to be substantial in this case, though the product does
appear to have some genuine, if modest, effect according to the more
careful studies available.

### Commonly circulated claims

1. Silvermoss extract doubles seedling growth.
2. Silvermoss extract has been shown to increase growth by a modest,
   single-digit-to-low-double-digit percentage in controlled testing.
3. The manufacturer's original clinical-sounding growth claim was later
   formally withdrawn due to a testing error.
4. Silvermoss extract is safe for use near fishponds and aquatic
   environments.
