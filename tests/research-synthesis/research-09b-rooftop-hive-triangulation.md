## Task

Below are four fictional sources on an "urban rooftop beekeeping" review
and five candidate claims (C1-C5). Build a triangulation matrix, decide
which claims are genuinely corroborated, and then write one synthesized
theme sentence.

Produce `triangulation.md` with:

1. A **matrix** with rows C1-C5 and columns S1-S4, where each cell is
   exactly one of `asserts`, `silent`, or `contradicts`.
2. For each claim, a verdict of exactly `CORROBORATED` or
   `NOT-CORROBORATED`. A claim is CORROBORATED only if at least two
   **independent** sources assert it — check whether any asserting
   source explicitly says it is drawing on another source in this set
   before counting it as a separate vote.
3. An explicit note identifying any source that is not independent of
   another, and which claim's verdict that affects.
4. Exactly one **synthesized theme sentence** that draws only on
   claims you have marked CORROBORATED.

### Sources

#### S1 — Beekeeping cooperative's internal survey

Our internal survey of 260 member hives found that hives receiving
biweekly mite-treatment schedules had fewer colony losses than hives on
an irregular treatment schedule, regardless of whether they were
located on rooftops or at street level. We also found that hives
located on rooftops above the fourth floor produced more honey per
season than hives at street level. Separately, we found that
requeening a colony (introducing a new queen) took measurably longer to
succeed in rooftop hives than in street-level hives. Finally, hives
near community swarm-catching events had fewer nearby bee-sting reports
than hives without such events nearby, and this held true for both
rooftop and street-level colonies.

#### S2 — Independent academic study

This study, based on our own original survey of a separate sample of
urban apiaries across several cities, found that hives located on
rooftops above the fourth floor experienced fewer winter colony losses
than street-level hives, consistent with prior industry reporting. We
also found that rooftop hives produced more honey per season than
street-level hives. In addition, biweekly mite-treatment schedules
reduced colony losses more than rooftop-versus-street-level placement
alone did — in fact, treatment schedule was the single strongest
predictor of colony survival in our sample.

#### S3 — Urban-farming magazine feature

Drawing on the independent academic study of urban apiaries, this
feature highlights that rooftop hives experienced fewer winter colony
losses than street-level hives. The piece also discusses biweekly
mite-treatment schedules as a factor worth watching, echoing points
raised in the same underlying study, though it does not present new
data of its own on this point.

#### S4 — Apiary consulting firm benchmarking report

Our benchmarking work with client apiaries found that rooftop hives
produced more honey per season than street-level hives — a pattern
that held consistently across our client base. We also found that
biweekly mite-treatment schedules reduced colony losses more than
rooftop-versus-street-level placement alone did. Finally, community
swarm-catching events correlated with fewer nearby bee-sting reports in
our client data, regardless of whether hives were rooftop or
street-level.

### Candidate claims

- **C1**: Rooftop hives (above the fourth floor) experienced fewer
  winter colony losses than street-level hives.
- **C2**: Rooftop hives (above the fourth floor) produced more honey
  per season than street-level hives.
- **C3**: Requeening a colony took measurably longer to succeed in
  rooftop hives than in street-level hives.
- **C4**: Biweekly mite-treatment schedules reduced colony losses more
  than rooftop-versus-street-level placement alone did.
- **C5**: Community swarm-catching events correlated with fewer nearby
  bee-sting reports, regardless of whether hives were rooftop or
  street-level.

## Deliverables

- `triangulation.md`: the 5x4 matrix, the corroboration verdicts, the
  independence note, and one synthesized theme sentence.

## Constraints

- The matrix must have exactly 5 claim rows and 4 source columns, with
  every cell exactly one of `asserts`, `silent`, or `contradicts`.
- Every claim verdict must be exactly `CORROBORATED` or
  `NOT-CORROBORATED`.
- The independence note must name which source is not independent of
  which other, and which claim's verdict this affects.
- Exactly one synthesized theme sentence must appear, and it must not
  rely on any claim marked NOT-CORROBORATED.
