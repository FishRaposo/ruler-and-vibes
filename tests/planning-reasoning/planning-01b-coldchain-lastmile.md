## Task

A regional food co-op currently runs **7 refrigerated delivery routes a
day** and needs to lock in how it will handle refrigerated last-mile
delivery. The options:

- **Contract courier**: $1,900/month flat for up to 8 daily routes, live
  in about a week, no vehicles or drivers owned, and a surcharge of $650
  per route per month for every route above 8.
- **Owned refrigerated fleet**: a supplier quotes $96,000 up front (two
  reefer vans plus fit-out) and about $2,300/month to run, roughly 4
  months to stand up, full ownership of the vehicles.
- **Leased vans plus gig drivers**: no purchase, about $15,000 of
  one-time setup (cold-chain retrofit, telematics, onboarding — 3 weeks),
  then about $1,100/month, with day-to-day dispatch and cold-chain checks
  falling on the co-op's one operations coordinator.

Write a decision memo for the co-op's general manager.

## Deliverables

- `decision.md` — must contain: a weighted decision matrix with at least
  4 criteria you choose and justify, one clear recommendation, and a
  risks-and-mitigations section for the recommended option.

## Constraints

- At most 600 words. Use the numbers given; state any additional
  assumptions explicitly.
