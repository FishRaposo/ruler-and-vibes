## Task

Hollowmere Brewing, a six-person craft brewery, needs a way to package
its beer into cans. It currently outputs about 600 cases a month. The
options:

- **Mobile canning contract**: a flat $430/month covers on-site canning
  up to 700 cases/month; above that the rate jumps to $780/month. No money
  up front, starts next month, the crew brings and runs the gear.
- **New in-house line**: a supplier quotes $34,000 up front plus about
  $520/month in consumables and upkeep, five months to install, full
  ownership and effectively unlimited throughput.
- **Refurbished line plus training**: about $6,800 to buy, refurbish, and
  train (four weeks), then roughly $175/month in consumables, with upkeep
  falling on the brewery's one mechanically-inclined staffer.

Write a decision memo for the brewery's owner.

## Deliverables

- `decision.md` — must contain: a weighted decision matrix with at least
  4 criteria you choose and justify, one clear recommendation, and a
  risks-and-mitigations section for the recommended option.

## Constraints

- At most 600 words. Use the numbers given; state any additional
  assumptions explicitly.
