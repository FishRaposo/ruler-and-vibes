## Task

Flow description (fictional meditation app **Hush**):

1. App install → first launch → full-screen "Transform your mind" hero
   with a single "Get Started" button.
2. Tap "Get Started" → a 7-day free trial pitch with the price
   "After trial: $14.99/month" in 11px grey text below the large
   "Start Free Trial" button.
3. No "Skip," "X," or back affordance on this screen — the only
   tap target is "Start Free Trial."
4. On tap: Apple/Google pay sheet slides up for Face ID confirmation.
5. On confirm: a 6-screen "pick your interests" wizard with no
   progress indicator (e.g., "3 of 6") and no skip.
6. After wizard: the home screen loads — but every meditation except
   one 90-second sample is locked behind the $14.99/month
   subscription that was just started without any free content
   surfaced during the trial period.
