## Task

You are copyediting the passage below for **internal consistency**.
There is no external style card — instead, several items are spelled
or formatted inconsistently within the passage itself, along
different axes (a name, a compound term, a unit).

**TIE-BREAK RULE:** for each inconsistent item, normalize every
occurrence to the form used at its FIRST occurrence in the passage.
Do not introduce any other change — leave every already-consistent
sentence exactly as written.

**PASSAGE (to be copyedited):**

> Adaeze Nwosu took charge of the letterpress shop's Saturday
> make-ready clinic last autumn, and the room filled up almost at once.
> Adaeze Nwosu rearranged the type cabinets so that the most-used fonts
> sat nearest the composing stone, and volunteers now pull a proof of
> every forme before it goes on press, treating a paper weight of
> exactly 120 gsm as the house stock for the teaching runs. The
> make-ready clinic, nine volunteers in all, now cycles through the two
> presses each session without the jams that used to eat the afternoon.
>
> Adeaze Nwosu also redrew the beginners' guide so newcomers could set
> a forme without a mentor hovering, and by the third week the whole
> makeready clinic could lock up a chase unaided. Type that once sat
> unsorted in the wrong drawers each week is now filed back before the
> next session opens. A later reorder for a poster job brought in one
> batch of cover stock at 118 grams per square metre, which the crew set
> aside for trimming rather than the teaching runs.
>
> Adaeze Nwosu says the next step is opening a second make-ready clinic
> on weeknights, though that group keeps a different pace and may want
> its own set of clinic rules rather than a copy of this one. Adaeze
> Nwosu also plans to pin the 120 gsm figure to a card above the paper
> shelf so no one has to guess.

## Deliverables

- `corrected.txt` — the fully copyedited passage, with every
  inconsistent item normalized to its first-occurrence form and every
  already-consistent sentence left untouched.

## Constraints

- Detect every inconsistency axis in the passage yourself; none is
  labeled for you.
- Apply the first-occurrence tie-break rule exactly — do not normalize
  to a majority form, a "more correct" form, or any convention external
  to this passage.
- Do not alter anything beyond what the detected inconsistencies
  require.
