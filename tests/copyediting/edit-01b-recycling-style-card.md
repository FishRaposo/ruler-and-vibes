## Task

You are copyediting a short internal bulletin against your
organization's **STYLE CARD**. Apply every rule that genuinely
applies, exactly as written. Do not introduce any change the style
card does not license — some sentences already comply and must be
left exactly as written.

**STYLE CARD:**

> 1. Use "backup" (one word, no hyphen), never "back-up."
> 2. Use "coordinator," never "co-ordinator."
> 3. Spell out whole numbers below 10 in body text (e.g. "seven," not
>    "7").
> 4. Use the serial (Oxford) comma in lists of three or more items.

**PASSAGE (to be copyedited):**

> The recycling program relies on a nightly back-up of the sorting
> logs, and the depot co-ordinator reviews them each morning. Six new
> drop-off stations opened this month, and each one received a signage
> kit, a scale, and a bin liner. The route now covers 9 neighborhoods
> across the district, though the back-up server for the compost data
> will not be replaced until spring.
>
> Residents can now report a missed pickup by phone, app or web form,
> and most reports are resolved within a day. The depot manager asked
> for 4 volunteers to help audit the new back-up schedule before it
> takes effect city-wide. She also asked whether the co-ordinator rota
> needed a refresh, since some of the older shifts still list the
> retired glass-only lane.

## Deliverables

- `corrected.txt` — the fully copyedited passage, applying every
  style-card rule that genuinely applies and leaving every
  already-compliant sentence exactly as written.

## Constraints

- Apply the style card mechanically. Do not rewrite, reorder, or
  rephrase anything beyond what the four rules require.
- Do not "fix" anything that already complies with the style card.
