## Task

You are copyediting a short community bulletin against your
organization's **STYLE CARD**. Apply every rule that genuinely
applies, exactly as written. Do not introduce any change the style
card does not license — some sentences already comply and must be
left exactly as written.

**STYLE CARD:**

> 1. Use "walkthrough" (one word, no hyphen), never "walk-through."
> 2. Use "catalog," never "catalogue."
> 3. Spell out whole numbers below 10 in body text (e.g. "eight," not
>    "8").
> 4. Use the serial (Oxford) comma in lists of three or more items.

**PASSAGE (to be copyedited):**

> The Larchwood tool library ran its first hands-on walk-through for
> new volunteers last weekend, using the printed catalogue of shared
> equipment. Five members signed up for the session, and each one
> received a key fob, a safety card, and a locker. The workshop stocks
> 6 workbenches along the north wall, though the walk-through for the
> lathe room will not be scheduled until the fall.
>
> Our loan desk now takes reservations by phone, kiosk or the website,
> and most items ship out within a day. The coordinator asked for 4
> mentors to help lead the next walk-through before the busy season
> starts. She also wondered whether the equipment catalogue needed
> reprinting, since a few of the older pages still list tools the
> library has since retired.

## Deliverables

- `corrected.txt` — the fully copyedited passage, applying every
  style-card rule that genuinely applies and leaving every
  already-compliant sentence exactly as written.

## Constraints

- Apply the style card mechanically. Do not rewrite, reorder, or
  rephrase anything beyond what the four rules require.
- Do not "fix" anything that already complies with the style card.
