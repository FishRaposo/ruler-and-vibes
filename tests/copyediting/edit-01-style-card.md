## Task

You are copyediting a short internal passage against your
organization's **STYLE CARD**. Apply every rule that genuinely
applies, exactly as written. Do not introduce any change the style
card does not license — some sentences already comply and must be
left exactly as written.

**STYLE CARD:**

> 1. Use "onboarding" (one word, no hyphen), never "on-boarding."
> 2. Use "email," never "e-mail."
> 3. Spell out whole numbers below 10 in body text (e.g. "eight," not
>    "8").
> 4. Use the serial (Oxford) comma in lists of three or more items.

**PASSAGE (to be copyedited):**

> The onboarding team met with the client on-boarding lead last week to
> review the new e-mail templates. Seven new hires joined the roster,
> and each one received a laptop, a badge, and a mentor. The rollout
> covers 8 departments across the building, though on-boarding for the
> finance group will not start until next quarter.
>
> Our support desk now answers questions by email, chat or phone, and
> most tickets close within a day. The manager asked for 3 volunteers
> to help test the new on-boarding checklist before it goes live
> company-wide. She also asked whether the e-mail template library
> needed a refresh, since some of the older messages still reference
> the retired ticketing system.

## Deliverables

- `corrected.txt` — the fully copyedited passage, applying every
  style-card rule that genuinely applies and leaving every
  already-compliant sentence exactly as written.

## Constraints

- Apply the style card mechanically. Do not rewrite, reorder, or
  rephrase anything beyond what the four rules require.
- Do not "fix" anything that already complies with the style card.
