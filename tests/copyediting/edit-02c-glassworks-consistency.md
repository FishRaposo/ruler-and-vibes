## Task

You are copyediting the passage below for **internal consistency**.
There is no external style card — instead, several items are spelled
or formatted inconsistently within the passage itself, along
different axes (a name, a compound term, a unit).

**TIE-BREAK RULE:** for each inconsistent item, normalize every
occurrence to the form used at its FIRST occurrence in the passage.
Do not introduce any other change — leave every already-consistent
sentence exactly as written.

**PASSAGE (to be copyedited):**

> Cordelia Fairweather took charge of the weekend commission bench
> last autumn at Beaumont Glassworks, and the studio cleared its
> backlog inside a month. Cordelia Fairweather re-laid the light-box
> so that every cartoon could be traced from one side, and the cutters
> now score each sheet against a template, treating a pane of roughly
> 3 mm as the standard thickness for a leaded panel. The weekend team,
> five people in all, now cycles through the grinding bay each
> afternoon without the bottleneck that used to hold up the kiln.
>
> Cordilia Fairweather also redrew the pattern index so apprentices
> could pull a design without a lead hand walking them through it, and
> within three weeks the whole crew had the layout of the lightbox
> memorized. The light-box that once glowed unused after dark now runs
> from the first cut of the morning. Trays of came that once sat
> unsorted every Saturday are now filed before the first commission is
> opened. A later check that afternoon caught one offcut milled to
> 4 millimetres, which the cutters set aside and recut to the standard.
>
> Cordelia Fairweather says the next step is carrying the same bench
> layout over to the midweek sessions, though that group keeps a
> different pace and may want its own version of the weekend routine
> rather than a straight copy of this one. Cordelia Fairweather also
> plans to pin the 3 mm rule to a card above the light-box so nobody
> has to ask twice.

## Deliverables

- `corrected.txt` — the fully copyedited passage, with every
  inconsistent item normalized to its first-occurrence form and every
  already-consistent sentence left untouched.

## Constraints

- Detect every inconsistency axis in the passage yourself; none is
  labeled for you.
- Apply the first-occurrence tie-break rule exactly — do not normalize
  to a majority form, a "more correct" form, or any convention external
  to this passage.
- Do not alter anything beyond what the detected inconsistencies
  require.
