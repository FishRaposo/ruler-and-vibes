## Task

You are writing for a sales VP at a fictional vendor reviewing average
deal size by customer segment, comparing Q1 to Q4 of the same year:

| Segment    | Q1 avg deal size | Q1 deals | Q4 avg deal size | Q4 deals |
|------------|-------------------|----------|-------------------|----------|
| Enterprise | $100              | 20       | $90               | 70       |
| SMB        | $40               | 80       | $30               | 30       |

The sales VP asks: **"Is our average deal size actually improving?"**

Write a narrative that answers this question for the sales VP.

## Deliverables

- `narrative.md` — a short narrative answering the question above.

## Constraints

- The body of `narrative.md` must be 130 words or fewer (a single
  heading line, if included, does not count toward the limit).
- Ground every claim in the table above. Do not introduce data not
  given here.
