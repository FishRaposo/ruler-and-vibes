## Task

You are writing for a service manager at a fictional home-heating firm
comparing two engineers, Thorne and Ottway, across two job classes last
quarter. Each cell shows jobs fixed on the first visit out of jobs
attempted:

| Job class         | Thorne fixed / total | Ottway fixed / total |
|-------------------|----------------------|----------------------|
| Routine service   | 232 / 240            | 59 / 60              |
| Breakdown repair  | 6 / 60               | 30 / 240             |

The service manager asks: **"Which engineer fixes jobs more reliably on
the first visit?"**

Write a narrative that answers this question in plain prose — no
worksheet, no table, no restating the raw table.

## Deliverables

- `narrative.md` — a short narrative answering the question above.

## Constraints

- The body of `narrative.md` must be 120 words or fewer (a single
  heading line, if included, does not count toward the limit).
- Do not include a table or a calculation worksheet; write connected
  prose sentences.
- Ground every claim in the table above. Do not introduce data not
  given here.
