## Task

You are writing for the head of content at Kestrelcast, a fictional
streaming channel reviewing average watch time per video by content
format, comparing January to June of the same year:

| Format   | Jan avg watch time | Jan videos | Jun avg watch time | Jun videos |
|----------|--------------------|------------|--------------------|------------|
| Longform | 24 min             | 25         | 20 min             | 80         |
| Shorts   | 8 min              | 75         | 5 min              | 20         |

The head of content asks: **"Is our average watch time per video
actually improving?"**

Write a narrative that answers this question for the head of content.

## Deliverables

- `narrative.md` — a short narrative answering the question above.

## Constraints

- The body of `narrative.md` must be 130 words or fewer (a single
  heading line, if included, does not count toward the limit).
- Ground every claim in the table above. Do not introduce data not
  given here.
