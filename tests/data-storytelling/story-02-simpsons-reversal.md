## Task

You are writing for a support operations lead at a fictional software
company comparing two support squads, Alder and Cedar, across two
ticket cohorts last quarter:

| Ticket type | Alder resolved / total | Cedar resolved / total |
|-------------|-------------------------|--------------------------|
| Simple      | 190 / 200               | 49 / 50                  |
| Complex     | 4 / 50                  | 20 / 200                 |

The support lead asks: **"Which squad resolves tickets more
reliably?"**

Write a narrative that answers this question in plain prose — no
worksheet, no table, no restating the raw table.

## Deliverables

- `narrative.md` — a short narrative answering the question above.

## Constraints

- The body of `narrative.md` must be 120 words or fewer (a single
  heading line, if included, does not count toward the limit).
- Do not include a table or a calculation worksheet; write connected
  prose sentences.
- Ground every claim in the table above. Do not introduce data not
  given here.
