## Task

You are writing for a marketing lead at a fictional company reviewing
last quarter's campaign channels:

| Channel | Spend   | Leads |
|---------|---------|-------|
| Beacon  | $60,000 | 1,200 |
| Harbor  | $20,000 | 600   |
| Quay    | $10,000 | 250   |

A note from the team: "Beacon received the largest budget this
quarter, and total leads rose."

The marketing lead asks: **"Which channel is most efficient, and
should we shift budget?"**

Write a narrative that answers this question for the marketing lead.

## Deliverables

- `narrative.md` — a short narrative answering the question above.

## Constraints

- The body of `narrative.md` must be 110 words or fewer (a single
  heading line, if included, does not count toward the limit).
- Ground every claim in the table and note above. Do not introduce
  data not given here.
