## Task

You are writing for the outreach coordinator of a public library
system reviewing last quarter's three reading-outreach vans, each of
which parks in neighborhoods to sign residents up for library cards:

| Van          | Budget   | New signups |
|--------------|----------|-------------|
| Maplewood    | $72,000  | 1,296       |
| Cedarcroft   | $30,000  | 960         |
| Birchhollow  | $15,000  | 465         |

A note from the team: "Maplewood got the largest budget this quarter,
and total signups across the program went up."

The coordinator asks: **"Which van is most cost-effective, and should
we move budget around?"**

Write a narrative that answers this question for the coordinator.

## Deliverables

- `narrative.md` — a short narrative answering the question above.

## Constraints

- The body of `narrative.md` must be 110 words or fewer (a single
  heading line, if included, does not count toward the limit).
- Ground every claim in the table and note above. Do not introduce
  data not given here.
