## Task

You are writing for a quality manager at a fictional appliance
manufacturer. Last quarter's warranty data for its two assembly
plants:

| Plant     | Units shipped | Warranty claims |
|-----------|---------------|-----------------|
| Brantwick | 35,000        | 210             |
| Solmere   | 7,000         | 98              |

The quality manager asks: **"Which plant has the worse warranty
problem, and why?"**

Write a narrative that answers this question for the quality manager.

## Deliverables

- `narrative.md` — a short narrative (not a table, not a bulleted
  list of raw numbers) answering the question above.

## Constraints

- The body of `narrative.md` must be 90 words or fewer (a single
  heading line, if included, does not count toward the limit).
- Ground every claim in the table above. Do not introduce data not
  given here.
