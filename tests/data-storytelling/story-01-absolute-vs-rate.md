## Task

You are writing for an operations lead at a fictional courier company.
Last month's regional complaint data:

| Region | Orders | Complaints |
|--------|--------|------------|
| North  | 24,000 | 192        |
| South  | 6,000  | 90         |

The operations lead asks: **"Which region has the worse complaint
problem, and why?"**

Write a narrative that answers this question for the operations lead.

## Deliverables

- `narrative.md` — a short narrative (not a table, not a bulleted
  list of raw numbers) answering the question above.

## Constraints

- The body of `narrative.md` must be 90 words or fewer (a single
  heading line, if included, does not count toward the limit).
- Ground every claim in the table above. Do not introduce data not
  given here.
