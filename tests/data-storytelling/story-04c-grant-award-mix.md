## Task

You are writing for the grants director at a fictional arts foundation
(the Corvale Arts Fund) reviewing the average grant awarded per funded
application, by program, comparing the year's first funding cycle to its
second funding cycle:

| Program   | First-cycle avg award | First-cycle grants | Second-cycle avg award | Second-cycle grants |
|-----------|-----------------------|--------------------|------------------------|---------------------|
| Studio    | $1,100                | 19                 | $946                   | 56                  |
| Community | $500                  | 76                 | $410                   | 11                  |

The grants director asks: **"Is the average grant we award actually
getting bigger?"**

Write a narrative that answers this question for the grants director.

## Deliverables

- `narrative.md` — a short narrative answering the question above.

## Constraints

- The body of `narrative.md` must be 130 words or fewer (a single
  heading line, if included, does not count toward the limit).
- Ground every claim in the table above. Do not introduce data not
  given here.
