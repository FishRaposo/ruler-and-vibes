## Task

You are writing for a donor-safety lead at a fictional regional blood
service. Last quarter's data for its two donation sites:

| Site      | Donations | Adverse reactions |
|-----------|-----------|-------------------|
| Aldermere | 42,000    | 294               |
| Fenwick   | 12,000    | 156               |

The donor-safety lead asks: **"Which site has the worse adverse-reaction
problem, and why?"**

Write a narrative that answers this question for the donor-safety lead.

## Deliverables

- `narrative.md` — a short narrative (not a table, not a bulleted
  list of raw numbers) answering the question above.

## Constraints

- The body of `narrative.md` must be 90 words or fewer (a single
  heading line, if included, does not count toward the limit).
- Ground every claim in the table above. Do not introduce data not
  given here.
