## Task

You are writing for a public-health program manager at a fictional
county health department reviewing last quarter's three flu-vaccination
pop-up clinics:

| Clinic    | Cost    | Doses |
|-----------|---------|-------|
| Marlcroft | $30,000 | 360   |
| Tarnwick  | $14,000 | 266   |
| Redmarsh  | $8,000  | 112   |

A note from the coordinator: "Marlcroft received the largest budget
this quarter, and total doses administered rose."

The program manager asks: **"Which clinic is most efficient, and should
we reallocate budget?"**

Write a narrative that answers this question for the program manager.

## Deliverables

- `narrative.md` — a short narrative answering the question above.

## Constraints

- The body of `narrative.md` must be 110 words or fewer (a single
  heading line, if included, does not count toward the limit).
- Ground every claim in the table and note above. Do not introduce
  data not given here.
