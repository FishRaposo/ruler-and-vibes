## Task

A rectangular storage unit floor measures 56 m long and 33 m wide. A
racking installation crew of 5 workers spent 3 days on site measuring
and planning the unit. While there, the crew also measured an unrelated
loading-dock ramp running 140 m from the unit to the truck bay, which
would cost 18 credits per metre to resurface. The crew's site notes
also record the storage unit's diagonal brace measurement as 64 m.
Bumper rail for the unit's perimeter costs 9 credits per metre.

Answer all three questions, showing the arithmetic behind each:

- **Q1**: What is the storage unit's floor area?
- **Q2**: What is the total length of bumper rail needed to line the
  unit's full perimeter, and what is the total bumper rail cost?
- **Q3**: Is the crew's recorded diagonal brace measurement of 64 m
  consistent with the unit's stated side lengths? If not, what is the
  correct diagonal?

## Deliverables

- `LAYOUT.md` — must contain, in this order:
  - Q1's answer with the arithmetic shown.
  - Q2's answer: bumper rail length and bumper rail cost, each with
    arithmetic shown.
  - Q3's answer: a clear verdict on the diagonal's consistency, the
    correct diagonal if the stated one is wrong, and the arithmetic
    (e.g. Pythagorean check) behind that verdict.
  - An explicit note identifying which of the given figures, if any,
    are irrelevant to Q1–Q3 and were therefore not used.

## Constraints

- At most 350 words (whole file, `wc -w`).
- Use only the figures actually needed for each requested quantity; do
  not fold unrelated figures into a requested total.
