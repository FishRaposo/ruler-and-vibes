## Task

A river festival needs to ferry 437 guests across a river using a single
chartered ferry.

- The ferry is rated for 30 people total, but 2 crew members must be
  aboard on every crossing, leaving **at most 28 guests per crossing**.
- The festival pays a **charter fee of 45 coins per crossing**, except
  every 5th crossing (the 5th, 10th, 15th, and so on) is free of
  charter fee.
- **Fuel is billed separately at cost on every crossing, including
  charter-free ones**: 3.5 liters per crossing at 6 coins per liter.
- The festival pays both the charter fees and the fuel costs.

Answer the following, showing brief working for each:

- **Q1**: What is the minimum number of crossings needed to move all
  437 guests?
- **Q2**: What is the total charter fee paid across all crossings
  (remembering every 5th crossing waives the charter fee)?
- **Q3**: What is the total fuel cost across all crossings, the grand
  total (charter fees + fuel), and the cost per guest (grand total
  divided by 437 guests, rounded to 2 decimal places)?
- **Q4**: Suppose, hypothetically, a crewless ferry could carry the
  full 30 guests per crossing instead of 28. How many crossings would
  that take, and how many crossings fewer is that compared to your Q1
  answer?

All numbers needed are stated above; nothing external is required.
