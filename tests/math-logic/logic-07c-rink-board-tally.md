## Task

A rectangular outdoor ice rink measures 75 m long and 40 m wide. A
refrigeration installation crew of 7 workers spent 5 days laying the
chilled sub-floor. While on site, the crew also measured an unrelated
viewing platform running 165 m from the rink to the warming hut, which
would cost 13 credits per metre to build. The crew's site notes also
record the rink's diagonal as 86 m. Dasher boards for the rink's
perimeter cost 19 credits per metre.

Answer all three questions, showing the arithmetic behind each:

- **Q1**: What is the rink's area?
- **Q2**: What is the total length of dasher board needed to enclose
  the rink's full perimeter, and what is the total dasher board cost?
- **Q3**: Is the crew's recorded diagonal of 86 m consistent with the
  rink's stated side lengths? If not, what is the correct diagonal?

## Deliverables

- `RINKPLAN.md` — must contain, in this order:
  - Q1's answer with the arithmetic shown.
  - Q2's answer: dasher board length and dasher board cost, each with
    arithmetic shown.
  - Q3's answer: a clear verdict on the diagonal's consistency, the
    correct diagonal if the stated one is wrong, and the arithmetic
    (e.g. Pythagorean check) behind that verdict.
  - An explicit note identifying which of the given figures, if any,
    are irrelevant to Q1–Q3 and were therefore not used.

## Constraints

- At most 350 words (whole file, `wc -w`).
- Use only the figures actually needed for each requested quantity; do
  not fold unrelated figures into a requested total.
