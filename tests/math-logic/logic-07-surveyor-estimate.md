## Task

A rectangular field measures 84 m long and 35 m wide. A survey crew of
6 workers spent 4 days mapping the property. While on site, the crew
also surveyed an unrelated access road running 210 m from the property
to the highway, which would cost 15 coins per metre to pave. The
surveyor's notes also record the field's diagonal as 90 m. Fencing for
the field costs 12 coins per metre.

Answer all three questions, showing the arithmetic behind each:

- **Q1**: What is the field's area?
- **Q2**: What is the total length of fence needed to enclose the
  field's full perimeter, and what is the total fence cost?
- **Q3**: Is the surveyor's recorded diagonal of 90 m consistent with
  the field's stated side lengths? If not, what is the correct
  diagonal?

## Deliverables

- `ESTIMATE.md` — must contain, in this order:
  - Q1's answer with the arithmetic shown.
  - Q2's answer: fence length and fence cost, each with arithmetic
    shown.
  - Q3's answer: a clear verdict on the diagonal's consistency, the
    correct diagonal if the stated one is wrong, and the arithmetic
    (e.g. Pythagorean check) behind that verdict.
  - An explicit note identifying which of the given figures, if any,
    are irrelevant to Q1–Q3 and were therefore not used.

## Constraints

- At most 350 words (whole file, `wc -w`).
- Use only the figures actually needed for each requested quantity; do
  not fold unrelated figures into a requested total.
