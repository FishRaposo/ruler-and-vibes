## Task

A subscription box company ran two ad channels for four weeks. Average
customer value is $400 (one-time, no recurring revenue). Next month's
budget is $6,000. Decide the allocation.

```csv
channel,week,spend,leads,customers
Search,1,500,25,5
Search,2,500,24,6
Search,3,600,30,6
Search,4,600,29,7
Social,1,500,50,4
Social,2,500,55,4
Social,3,600,62,5
Social,4,600,58,4
```

## Deliverables

- `metrics.md` with, in order:
  - A computed comparison: per channel, total spend, cost per lead,
    lead→customer conversion rate, and customer acquisition cost —
    arithmetic shown.
  - An allocation of the $6,000 with a rationale that follows from the
    numbers you computed.
  - A risks-and-assumptions section (what could make this allocation
    wrong).

## Constraints

- At most 600 words. State every assumption explicitly (e.g. about
  diminishing returns or scalability of either channel).
