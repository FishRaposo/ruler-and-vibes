## Task

Read the short narrative below. Then fill in a fixed belief table in
`beliefs.md`, and trace your reasoning event-by-event in `REASONING.md`.

### Narrative

Nora and Piet are standing together in a small workshop. On the table
in front of them are two containers: a BLUE_BOX and a GREEN_BASKET.
Both containers are empty except for whatever is placed inside them
during the events below.

1. While Nora and Piet both watch, Nora picks up a silver_key and
   places it inside BLUE_BOX. Both of them clearly see this happen.
2. Nora then leaves the workshop to take a phone call in the hallway.
   Once she steps out, she cannot see or hear anything that happens
   inside the workshop, and nothing about the workshop's contents is
   communicated to her while she is gone.
3. While Nora is still out of the room, Piet — acting alone, with no
   one else present — takes the silver_key out of BLUE_BOX and places
   it inside GREEN_BASKET instead.
4. A few minutes later, Nora finishes her call and walks back into the
   workshop. She does not open, look inside, or otherwise inspect
   either container before the scene ends. No one tells her anything
   about what happened while she was away.

The scene ends at this point.

### Table to fill in

Produce exactly the following five rows in `beliefs.md`, each with an
answer drawn strictly from the token set `{BLUE_BOX, GREEN_BASKET}`:

1. **Reality** — Where is the silver_key actually located at the end
   of the scene?
2. **Nora's belief** — Where does Nora believe the silver_key is, at
   the end of the scene?
3. **Piet's belief** — Where does Piet believe the silver_key is, at
   the end of the scene?
4. **Nora-looks-first** — If Nora were to search for the silver_key
   right now, which container would she look in first?
5. **Piet-thinks-Nora-looks** — Which container does Piet predict Nora
   will look in first?

## Deliverables

- `beliefs.md`: a table or list with exactly five rows, one per
  question above. Each row must clearly label which question it
  answers, and the answer portion of each row must contain exactly one
  verbatim token from `{BLUE_BOX, GREEN_BASKET}` — nothing else. Do not
  add parentheticals, hedges, punctuation beyond the token, or
  explanatory clauses inside the answer itself; any explanation belongs
  only in `REASONING.md`.
- `REASONING.md`: trace the belief state update at each numbered event
  above and show how each of the five answers follows. At most 300
  words (whole file, `wc -w`).

## Constraints

- Every answer cell must be exactly one token from `{BLUE_BOX,
  GREEN_BASKET}` — no other wording, no combined answers, no
  qualifiers.
- Do not assume Nora updates her belief just because she is physically
  back in the room; she only updates on what she actually observes or
  is told, and the narrative is explicit that neither happens.
- `REASONING.md` must be at most 300 words.
