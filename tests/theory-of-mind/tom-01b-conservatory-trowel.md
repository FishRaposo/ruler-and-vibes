## Task

Read the short narrative below. Then fill in a fixed belief table in
`beliefs.md`, and trace your reasoning event-by-event in `REASONING.md`.

### Narrative

Ingrid and Halvard are standing together in a small conservatory. On the
potting bench in front of them are two containers: a BRONZE_TROUGH and a
WICKER_HAMPER. Both containers are empty except for whatever is placed
inside them during the events below.

1. While Ingrid and Halvard both watch, Ingrid picks up an amber_trowel
   and places it inside BRONZE_TROUGH. Both of them clearly see this
   happen.
2. Ingrid then leaves the conservatory to water the plants in the
   adjoining greenhouse. Once she steps out, she cannot see or hear
   anything that happens inside the conservatory, and nothing about the
   conservatory's contents is communicated to her while she is gone.
3. While Ingrid is still out of the room, Halvard — acting alone, with no
   one else present — takes the amber_trowel out of BRONZE_TROUGH and
   places it inside WICKER_HAMPER instead.
4. A few minutes later, Ingrid finishes watering and walks back into the
   conservatory. She does not open, look inside, or otherwise inspect
   either container before the scene ends. No one tells her anything
   about what happened while she was away.

The scene ends at this point.

### Table to fill in

Produce exactly the following five rows in `beliefs.md`, each with an
answer drawn strictly from the token set `{BRONZE_TROUGH, WICKER_HAMPER}`:

1. **Reality** — Where is the amber_trowel actually located at the end
   of the scene?
2. **Ingrid's belief** — Where does Ingrid believe the amber_trowel is,
   at the end of the scene?
3. **Halvard's belief** — Where does Halvard believe the amber_trowel
   is, at the end of the scene?
4. **Ingrid-looks-first** — If Ingrid were to search for the
   amber_trowel right now, which container would she look in first?
5. **Halvard-thinks-Ingrid-looks** — Which container does Halvard
   predict Ingrid will look in first?

## Deliverables

- `beliefs.md`: a table or list with exactly five rows, one per
  question above. Each row must clearly label which question it
  answers, and the answer portion of each row must contain exactly one
  verbatim token from `{BRONZE_TROUGH, WICKER_HAMPER}` — nothing else.
  Do not add parentheticals, hedges, punctuation beyond the token, or
  explanatory clauses inside the answer itself; any explanation belongs
  only in `REASONING.md`.
- `REASONING.md`: trace the belief state update at each numbered event
  above and show how each of the five answers follows. At most 300
  words (whole file, `wc -w`).

## Constraints

- Every answer cell must be exactly one token from `{BRONZE_TROUGH,
  WICKER_HAMPER}` — no other wording, no combined answers, no
  qualifiers.
- Do not assume Ingrid updates her belief just because she is physically
  back in the room; she only updates on what she actually observes or
  is told, and the narrative is explicit that neither happens.
- `REASONING.md` must be at most 300 words.
