## Task

You are ghostwriting a newsletter entry for **B. Halloran**, a
fictional newsletter author. Below are three short samples of
Halloran's writing, followed by a VOICE FINGERPRINT sheet naming the
measurable tics of the voice. Write a **new** entry, in Halloran's
voice, on the assigned topic.

**SAMPLE 1:**

> Rain.
>
> It hit the tin roof first, then the gutter, then the street. I stood
> under the awning and watched the bus go by without me. Nobody else
> waited. Just me, a paper cup, and the smell of wet asphalt. The cup
> was empty — I'd finished the coffee blocks ago. Still I held it. A
> man across the street lit a cigarette under his own awning, cupping
> the flame with both hands. We nodded at each other, two strangers
> holding small useless things against the weather.

**SAMPLE 2:**

> Ladders.
>
> The painters showed up at seven with three of them, aluminum, dented
> at the feet. They leaned them against the church without asking. By
> noon the steeple had a new coat of white. I watched from my window
> with a cold cup of tea. One painter sang under his breath — off-key,
> happy, unbothered by the height. His partner passed him a brush
> without looking. Some jobs need no more talk than that. By dusk the
> ladders were gone, and the steeple looked like it had never been old.

**SAMPLE 3:**

> Keys.
>
> I found a ring of them in the drawer, none labeled, all rusted at
> the teeth. My grandfather kept them for decades — for doors that no
> longer existed, most likely. I tried them anyway on the shed, the
> mailbox, the old trunk in the attic. None worked. Still I kept
> trying, one key at a time, patient as a locksmith with nowhere else
> to be. The trunk finally gave on the ninth key. Inside: a folded
> flag, a photograph, and nothing else worth keeping.

**VOICE FINGERPRINT (Halloran's measurable tics):**

> - Sentences average under 12 words each (terse).
> - Every piece opens with a one-word paragraph.
> - Never uses semicolons.
> - Uses exactly one em-dash aside per piece.
> - Favors concrete nouns over abstractions.

**Assigned topic:** a neighborhood bakery closing.

## Deliverables

- `entry.md` — a new 150-180 word newsletter entry, in Halloran's
  voice, on the assigned topic.

## Constraints

- `entry.md` must be 150-180 words inclusive (whole file, `wc -w`).
- `entry.md` must open with a one-word first paragraph.
- `entry.md` must contain zero semicolons and exactly one em-dash
  ("—").
- To keep sentence length cleanly countable, `entry.md` must not use
  any abbreviations (e.g. no "a.m.", "Mr.", "St."), decimals, or
  ellipses — the only sentence terminators allowed are plain
  sentence-final periods, exclamation points, and question marks.
- `entry.md` must stay on the assigned topic (the neighborhood bakery
  closing) and keep sentences concrete and short, matching the
  fingerprint rather than lapsing into long abstract sentimentality.
