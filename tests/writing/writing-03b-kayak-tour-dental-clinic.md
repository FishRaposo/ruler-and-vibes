## Task

Localize both texts below — English to Brazilian Portuguese, and
Brazilian Portuguese to English. These are marketing texts: the goal
is a version that reads as if originally written in the target
language, not a literal translation.

**Text A (EN → PT-BR):**

> **Paddle Loop: a one-day guided kayak tour.**
> Paddle harder, drift less. Join us at Otter Bend Landing on June
> 13, 2027, from 9 a.m. to 2 p.m. Early registration ends May 20: $99
> instead of $139, and spots are first come, first served — the
> first 12 paddlers also get a free dry bag. Bring any kayak — even
> a rental. Bring your A-game.

**Text B (PT-BR → EN):**

> **Clínica OdontoBem: seu sorriso sem enrolação.**
> Agende até 09/06/2027 e ganhe 20% de desconto no clareamento
> dental, com avaliação inicial inclusa e cancelamento grátis até 24
> horas antes da consulta. Fica a poucos passos da Lagoa Azul.
> Crianças de até 12 anos não pagam a avaliação.

Rules:

- Brand names ("Paddle Loop", "Clínica OdontoBem", "Lagoa Azul")
  stay as they are.
- Do NOT convert currencies or amounts; adapt only formatting and
  date conventions to the target locale.
- Idioms and slogans get idiomatic equivalents or transcreations,
  never word-for-word renderings.
- Every fact must survive: dates, times, prices, percentages, counts,
  conditions.

## Deliverables

- `localization.md` with two sections: `## Text A — PT-BR` and
  `## Text B — EN`.

## Constraints

- At most 350 words total. No translator's footnotes inside the
  texts themselves (put any necessary remarks in REASONING.md).
