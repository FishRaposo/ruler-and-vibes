## Task

The paragraph below announces that a community ceramics studio is
postponing its annual exhibition. Rewrite it three times:

1. **Formal** — for a report to the studio's grant funder.
2. **Friendly** — for the members' newsletter.
3. **One-sentence summary** — 25 words or fewer.

> Owing to the fact that the renovation of the studio's kiln room
> uncovered unforeseen structural complications of a foundational
> nature that require additional reinforcement work, it has been
> determined by the studio collective that the annual Open-Studio
> Exhibition, which had originally been scheduled to take place on
> October 4th, will now be deferred until November 22nd, and members
> who have already purchased advance tickets will be receiving a refund
> of 20% as a gesture of goodwill for the disruption that has resulted.

All four facts must survive every version: (a) the cause is a
foundation problem found during the kiln-room renovation, (b) the old
date was October 4, (c) the new date is November 22, (d) advance-ticket
holders get a 20% refund.

## Deliverables

- `rewrite.md` — the three versions under headings `## Formal`,
  `## Friendly`, `## Summary`.

## Constraints

- The summary is one sentence of at most 25 words. No new facts invented
  in any version.
