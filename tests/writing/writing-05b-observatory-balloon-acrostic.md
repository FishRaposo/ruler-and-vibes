## Task

Write an original piece of flash fiction — no source material is
supplied; this is pure generation — about an astronomer at a remote
mountain observatory who discovers a handwritten letter sealed inside
the crashed instrument pod of a weather balloon that drifted down onto
the observatory's roof.

The story must satisfy three simultaneous hard constraints:

1. **Acrostic.** The story must have **exactly 7 paragraphs**,
   separated by blank lines (so a reader can segment them
   unambiguously). Reading the first letter of each paragraph in
   order must spell the seven-letter word **ORBITAL**
   (O-R-B-I-T-A-L).
2. **Banned words.** The following five words must appear **zero
   times**, checked case-insensitively as whole words: `abruptly`,
   `learned`, `stillness`, `blackness`, `soul`. (A longer word or a
   differently-spelled variant that merely contains or echoes these
   letters — e.g. "soulmate," "soulful," "learnt" — does not count as
   a violation, but do not rely on that loophole; simply avoid the
   five words entirely.)
3. **Word cap.** The whole file must be 180-220 words inclusive.

Write continuous narrative prose only: no headings, no bullet or
numbered lists.

## Deliverables

- `story.md` — the complete flash-fiction story, structured as
  exactly 7 blank-line-separated paragraphs whose initial letters
  spell ORBITAL, containing none of the five banned words, 180-220
  words total (whole file, `wc -w`), about an astronomer who
  discovers a letter inside a fallen weather balloon's instrument
  pod.

## Constraints

- `story.md` must be 180-220 words inclusive (whole file, `wc -w`).
- Exactly 7 paragraphs, separated by blank lines, whose first
  alphabetic character in order spells O-R-B-I-T-A-L.
- Zero occurrences (case-insensitive, whole word) of: abruptly,
  learned, stillness, blackness, soul.
- No headings and no bullet/numbered list markers — continuous prose
  only.
