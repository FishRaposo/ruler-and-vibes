## Task

Explain **DNS propagation** to a small-business owner who runs an
online shop and has no technical background. They just migrated their
store to a new web host, and the migration confirmation email said:
"Your domain's DNS records have been updated — changes can take up to
24 hours to propagate worldwide, so some visitors may briefly be sent
to your old address." They want to know what that means, and whether
their store and any orders placed during that window are safe.

## Deliverables

- `explainer.md` — a titled piece of continuous prose (a single `#`
  title, no other headings, no bullet lists) between 260 and 380 words,
  containing exactly one extended analogy drawn from everyday life, and
  explicitly answering whether their store and orders are safe.

## Constraints

- No unexplained jargon: any technical term must be explained in the
  same sentence or dropped.
