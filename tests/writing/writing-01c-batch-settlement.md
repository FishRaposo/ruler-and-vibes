## Task

Explain **batch settlement** to a farmers'-market vendor who takes card
payments through a mobile card-reader app but has no technical
background. They just read a notification from the app saying "your
running total may take a few hours to catch up after the market closes
— transactions authorize immediately but settle in batches" and want to
know what that means and whether their money is safe.

## Deliverables

- `explainer.md` — a titled piece of continuous prose (a single `#`
  title, no other headings, no bullet lists) between 250 and 400 words,
  containing exactly one extended analogy drawn from everyday life, and
  explicitly answering whether their money is safe.

## Constraints

- No unexplained jargon: any technical term must be explained in the
  same sentence or dropped.
