## Task

Write an original piece of flash fiction — no source material is
supplied; this is pure generation — about a piano tuner who discovers
an old photograph hidden inside the soundboard of a concert hall's
grand piano.

The story must satisfy three simultaneous hard constraints:

1. **Acrostic.** The story must have **exactly 7 paragraphs**,
   separated by blank lines (so a reader can segment them
   unambiguously). Reading the first letter of each paragraph in
   order must spell the seven-letter word **HARMONY**
   (H-A-R-M-O-N-Y).
2. **Banned words.** The following five words must appear **zero
   times**, checked case-insensitively as whole words: `abruptly`,
   `understood`, `quiet`, `hush`, `soul`. (A longer word that merely
   contains these letters — e.g. "disquiet," "soulful," "soulless" —
   does not count as a violation, but do not rely on that loophole;
   simply avoid the five words entirely.)
3. **Word cap.** The whole file must be 190-230 words inclusive.

Write continuous narrative prose only: no headings, no bullet or
numbered lists.

## Deliverables

- `story.md` — the complete flash-fiction story, structured as
  exactly 7 blank-line-separated paragraphs whose initial letters
  spell HARMONY, containing none of the five banned words, 190-230
  words total (whole file, `wc -w`), about a piano tuner who
  discovers an old photograph hidden inside a piano's soundboard.

## Constraints

- `story.md` must be 190-230 words inclusive (whole file, `wc -w`).
- Exactly 7 paragraphs, separated by blank lines, whose first
  alphabetic character in order spells H-A-R-M-O-N-Y.
- Zero occurrences (case-insensitive, whole word) of: abruptly,
  understood, quiet, hush, soul.
- No headings and no bullet/numbered list markers — continuous prose
  only.
