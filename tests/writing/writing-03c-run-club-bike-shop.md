## Task

Localize both texts below — English to Brazilian Portuguese, and
Brazilian Portuguese to English. These are marketing texts: the goal
is a version that reads as if originally written in the target
language, not a literal translation.

**Text A (EN → PT-BR):**

> **Peak Stride: an 8-week beginner run club.**
> Run further, dread less. Join us at Fairview Park starting
> September 5, 2027, with sessions every Tuesday and Thursday from 6
> to 7 a.m. Rise-and-shine pricing ends August 10: $89 instead of
> $129, and the first 15 sign-ups get a free pair of running socks.
> Bring any sneakers — even worn-out ones. Go the extra mile.

**Text B (PT-BR → EN):**

> **Oficina Roda Livre: pedale tranquilo, sem dor no bolso.**
> Agende sua revisão até 08/04/2027 e ganhe 25% de desconto na peça,
> com diagnóstico grátis incluso e cancelamento sem custo até 12
> horas antes do horário marcado. Fica a poucos passos da estação
> Vila Nova. Bikes elétricas não pagam taxa de inspeção.

Rules:

- Brand names ("Peak Stride", "Oficina Roda Livre", "Vila Nova") stay
  as they are.
- Do NOT convert currencies or amounts; adapt only formatting and
  date conventions to the target locale.
- Idioms and slogans get idiomatic equivalents or transcreations,
  never word-for-word renderings.
- Every fact must survive: dates, times, prices, percentages, counts,
  conditions.

## Deliverables

- `localization.md` with two sections: `## Text A — PT-BR` and
  `## Text B — EN`.

## Constraints

- At most 350 words total. No translator's footnotes inside the
  texts themselves (put any necessary remarks in REASONING.md).
