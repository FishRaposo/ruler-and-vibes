## Task

Localize both texts below — English to Brazilian Portuguese, and
Brazilian Portuguese to English. These are marketing texts: the goal
is a version that reads as if originally written in the target
language, not a literal translation.

**Text A (EN → PT-BR):**

> **Golden Hour: a one-day outdoor photography workshop.**
> Shoot better, edit less. Join us in Riverside Park on April 12,
> 2027, from 7 a.m. to 3 p.m. Early-bird pricing ends March 15: $149
> instead of $199, and the first 20 sign-ups get a free lens-cleaning
> kit. Bring any camera — even your phone. Rain or shine.

**Text B (PT-BR → EN):**

> **Pousada Maré Alta: seu fim de semana sem dor de cabeça.**
> Reserve até 05/03/2027 e ganhe 15% de desconto na diária, com café
> da manhã incluso e cancelamento grátis até 48 horas antes do
> check-in. Fica a dois passos da praia de Ponta Verde. Crianças de
> até 6 anos não pagam.

Rules:

- Brand names ("Golden Hour", "Pousada Maré Alta", "Ponta Verde")
  stay as they are.
- Do NOT convert currencies or amounts; adapt only formatting and
  date conventions to the target locale.
- Idioms and slogans get idiomatic equivalents or transcreations,
  never word-for-word renderings.
- Every fact must survive: dates, times, prices, percentages, counts,
  conditions.

## Deliverables

- `localization.md` with two sections: `## Text A — PT-BR` and
  `## Text B — EN`.

## Constraints

- At most 350 words total. No translator's footnotes inside the
  texts themselves (put any necessary remarks in REASONING.md).
