## Task

Hand-write an SVG poster for a fictional event: **Windward Cup Sailing
Regatta, 5–7 June 2028, Saltspray Cove**. Bright, breezy, coastal, and
readable at a glance.

## Deliverables

- `poster.svg` — portrait orientation with `viewBox="0 0 640 960"`,
  containing the event name, the dates, and the venue as text, plus at
  least three distinct non-text visual elements (shapes, gradients,
  patterns).

## Constraints

- Pure SVG: no external images, fonts, stylesheets, or scripts.
- At most 160 lines. Must render in a browser opened from `file://`.
