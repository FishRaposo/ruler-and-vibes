## Task

Build a launch page for **Steepwell**, a fictional smart countertop tea
kettle (six preset steep programs, 1.2-litre capacity, heats to the
target temperature in about 90 seconds, $129 launch price). One file,
ready to open from `file://`.

## Deliverables

- `launch.html` — a complete page containing, in order:
  - a hero: headline, supporting subhead, and a primary CTA button;
  - three feature blocks (one per product strength you choose to
    highlight);
  - a social-proof section with two short fictional testimonials
    (name + one line each);
  - a closing CTA section repeating the action;
  - a footer with fictional contact info.

## Constraints

- Single file, inline CSS (inline JS allowed but not required). No
  external resources of any kind: no CDN, fonts, images, or icons —
  visuals via CSS and inline SVG only.
- Responsive: no horizontal scrolling at 375px wide, and the feature
  blocks must reflow (stacked on mobile, side-by-side on desktop).
- CTA buttons may link to `#`.
- At most 400 lines.
