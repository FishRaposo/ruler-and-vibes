## Task

Build a single-file, pure-CSS illustrated scene: **a campfire crackling
under a starry night sky**, with at least one continuous CSS animation
(the flames flickering, smoke curling upward, stars twinkling — your
choice).

## Deliverables

- `scene.html` — one file, inline CSS only.

## Constraints

- No JavaScript, no images of any kind (no `<img>`, no
  `background-image: url(...)`, no data URIs), no external resources.
  Gradients, shapes, shadows, and transforms only. Must work opened from
  `file://`.
